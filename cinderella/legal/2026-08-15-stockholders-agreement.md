# STOCKHOLDERS' AGREEMENT
## OF CINDERELLA CORP. (a Delaware corporation)

Dated as of **[____], 2026**, among **Cinderella Corp.** (the "**Company**"), **Norman C. de Silva** ("**Founder**"), the other founders listed on **Schedule 1** (Ankur Jain, Sunjay Mathews, Greg Kristof — the "**Other Founders**"), and the investors who join by signing (the "**Investors**"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in the Certificate of Incorporation.

## ARTICLE 1 — CAPITAL STRUCTURE (reference)
1.1 **Classes.** The Company has **Class A Common Stock (1 vote/share)** and **Class B Common Stock (10 votes/share)**, identical except as to voting and conversion per the Certificate.
1.2 **Holdings.**
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **Founder (Norman)** holds the **Class B Common Stock** — the Company's only super-voting stock.
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) **Other Founders** and **all Investors** hold **Class A Common Stock (1 vote/share)** only. No person other than the Founder holds or may hold Class B.
&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) **Auto-conversion.** Consistent with the Certificate, any Class B transferred other than by a Permitted Transfer automatically converts to Class A — so control cannot pass to the Other Founders or Investors.
1.3 **Investor stake.** The Investors hold, in aggregate, **15%** of the Company's outstanding stock (Class A), acquired in the Seed Round.

## ARTICLE 2 — INVESTOR ECONOMICS (Revenue-Share Right + permanent equity)
2.1 **Permanent Equity.** Each Investor's Class A Common is **permanent** and carries pro-rata dividend and liquidation rights equal to all other common (per the Certificate).
2.2 **Revenue-Share Right (temporary, additive).** In addition to Section 2.1, the Company shall pay the Investors, pro rata:
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **50%** of the Company's **Gross Cash Revenue** collected, until cumulative revenue-share payments equal **1.0×** the Investors' aggregate Purchase Price; then
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) **10%** of Gross Cash Revenue collected, until cumulative revenue-share payments equal **2.0×**;
&nbsp;&nbsp;whereupon **the Revenue-Share Right terminates.** These payments are **in addition to** (not credited against) the Investors' equity, yielding an illustrative **~13.47×** blended return.

> **⚠️ KEY STRUCTURAL ITEM (for Loeb).** Common stock under the current Certificate has **equal** dividend/distribution rights, so a revenue-share **preference cannot live in the common stock itself.** Document Section 2.2 as **either (a)** a **separate Revenue-Share Agreement** (contractual, alongside 15% Class A Common — recommended, clean cap table), **or (b)** a **Series Seed Preferred** authorized by charter amendment carrying the preference. Also confirm the tax/characterization of a **%-of-gross-revenue** payment to holders (revenue-based-financing / royalty flavor), and define **Gross Cash Revenue** precisely.

2.3 **Dividends.** Dividends on common, if any, are Board-declared and paid pro rata per share across Class A and Class B equally (per the Certificate). The Revenue-Share Right in 2.2 is separate from ordinary dividends.

## ARTICLE 3 — GOVERNANCE / CONTROL
3.1 **Control.** The Founder's Class B (10 votes/share) controls all matters submitted to stockholders and thereby the election of the Board. Investors and Other Founders have **no board seat and no control rights** except Section 3.3.
3.2 **Board.** The Board consists of **[3] directors [confirm — set in Bylaws]**, elected by stockholder vote (Founder-controlled).
3.3 **Investor protective provisions (limited).** The Company shall not, without the consent of a majority-in-interest of the Investors:
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) amend the Certificate, Bylaws, or this Agreement in a manner that **disproportionately and adversely** affects the Investors' economic or Revenue-Share rights; or
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) issue securities **senior to** the Investors' Revenue-Share Right.
_All other decisions rest with the Board/Founder._

## ARTICLE 4 — INVESTOR CAPITAL-RETURN RIGHT (the "Ankur Right")
4.1 **Grant.** On or after **January 1, 2027**, the **Investor Representative (Ankur Jain)** may, by written notice, cause the Company to **return to the Investors their undeployed/unreturned capital** (in whole or part, pro rata), **to the extent of available, undeployed cash** (the reserve — the plan deploys ~$952K of the initial close through December 2027 and holds the balance in reserve), if in his good-faith judgment the Company has **not made sufficient progress to responsibly continue deploying investor capital.**
4.2 **Standard.** "Sufficient progress" means **[recommended hybrid: failure to meet objective milestones (e.g., ≥1 signed school LOI, ≥1 attached lead celebrity, ≥1 streamer or SPV-capital term sheet) by [date], with the Investor Representative's good-faith judgment as backstop].** **[Confirm.]**
4.3 **Nature — return of capital, not redemption.** A return under this Article is a **return of capital** that reduces the Investors' unreturned capital and counts toward their Revenue-Share Right; **it does not, by itself, cancel or reduce the Investors' Shares.** **[If the parties intend returned capital to also reduce equity, that must be structured as a share redemption — separate mechanics, securities/tax implications; NOT assumed here.]**
4.4 **Limitation.** Limited to available/undeployed cash; does not require liquidating deployed assets or SPVs and does not guarantee full return.

## ARTICLE 5 — TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS
5.1 **Restriction.** No stockholder may transfer shares without (a) Board consent and (b) securities-law compliance; purported transfers in violation are void. (Founder Permitted Transfers of Class B are governed by the Certificate.)
5.2 **Right of First Refusal + Co-Sale.** The Company, then the other stockholders, have a ROFR on proposed transfers; Investors have co-sale (tag-along) rights on Founder/Other-Founder sales. **[Confirm.]**
5.3 **Drag-Along.** On a Board-approved sale of the Company, all stockholders participate and vote in favor on the same per-share terms. **[Confirm.]**
