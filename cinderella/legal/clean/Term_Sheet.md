### INVESTOR ECONOMICS

Investors receive two distinct economic rights:

**1. Permanent Equity.** 15% of the Company's outstanding capital stock, held as Class A Common Stock. This interest is permanent and participates pro rata in all dividends and distributions, and in the proceeds of any sale of the Company, without cap or expiration.

**2. Revenue-Share Right.** In addition to the equity above, and pursuant to a separate Revenue-Share Agreement, the Company will pay Investors:

- **50%** of the Company's Gross Cash Revenue, until Investors have received aggregate payments equal to **1.0×** their invested capital ($1,500,000); then
- **10%** of the Company's Gross Cash Revenue, until aggregate payments equal **2.0×** their invested capital ($3,000,000);
- after which the Revenue-Share Right terminates.

The Revenue-Share Right is paid **in addition to**, and is not credited against, the Investors' permanent equity. Payments are made quarterly, pro rata among Investors.

### CAPITAL STRUCTURE AND GOVERNANCE

**Dual-Class Common Stock.** The Company has two classes of common stock:

| Class | Votes Per Share | Holder |
|---|---|---|
| Class A Common | 1 | Investors, and all holders other than the Founder |
| Class B Common | 10 | Norman C. de Silva, exclusively |

Class B Common Stock converts automatically into Class A Common Stock upon any transfer other than a transfer to the Founder's estate-planning vehicle. Accordingly, voting control of the Company is held exclusively by the Founder and cannot be transferred.

**Board of Directors.** Three directors, elected by stockholder vote. Investors do not receive a board seat or observer right.

**Voting.** Class A and Class B vote together as a single class. Investors' shares carry one vote per share.

**Investor Protective Provisions.** The Company will not, without the consent of holders of a majority of the Investor shares:

1. amend its charter or governing agreements in a manner that disproportionately and adversely affects the Investors; or
2. issue any security senior in payment priority to the Revenue-Share Right.

---

### INVESTOR RIGHTS

**Information Rights.** Investors receive annual financial statements within 120 days of fiscal year end, applicable annual tax forms, and written business updates not less than annually.

**Return of Capital.** Beginning January 1, 2027, the Investor Representative may require the Company to return undeployed capital to Investors, pro rata, from the Company's available reserve, if the Company has not achieved sufficient operating progress to warrant continued deployment. Sufficient progress is measured by the Company's achievement of at least two of the following:

1. an executed agreement or letter of intent with at least one Division I institution;
2. an executed agreement with at least one lead celebrity; and
3. an executed term sheet or definitive agreement with a distribution partner or capital partner for a school-level special purpose vehicle.

**Investor Representative.** Ankur Jain will serve as Investor Representative, acting on behalf of the Investors with respect to the return-of-capital right and the protective provisions.

---

### TRANSFER AND LIQUIDITY

**Transfer Restrictions.** Shares are restricted securities and may not be transferred absent registration or an available exemption, and are subject to a Company right of first refusal and board consent.

**Co-Sale.** Investors may participate pro rata in any sale of shares by the Founder to a third party.

**Drag-Along.** Upon a board-approved sale of the Company, all stockholders participate on the same per-share terms.

### THE COMPANY

Cinderella Corp. is the parent company behind *Making Cinderella*, a sports entertainment franchise that pairs high-profile celebrities with underdog Division I basketball programs and captures each season as a premium documentary series. The Company owns the franchise intellectual property, identifies and contracts schools and talent, structures and holds interests in school-level special purpose vehicles, and provides centralized media and commercial capability across the portfolio.

**Company Economics Per School.** 40% of SPV equity, a one-time franchise fee of 5% on SPV capital called, and a 2.5% annual management fee on SPV cost.

**Leadership.** Norman C. de Silva, Founder and Chief Executive Officer. Sunjay Mathews, Day 1 Partner. Ankur Jain and Greg Kristof, Strategic Advisors.
