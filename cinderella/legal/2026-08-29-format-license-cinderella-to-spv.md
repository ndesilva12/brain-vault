## 1. DEFINITIONS

**"Format"** means the *Making Cinderella* television and audiovisual format created and owned by Licensor, including its concept, structure, narrative architecture, production methodology, format bible, and associated know-how — pairing a celebrity with an underdog Division I basketball program, funding the roster through private capital, and capturing the season as a documentary series.

**"Franchise IP"** means the Format together with the Marks, all format bibles and development materials, all derivative and spin-off rights, and all other intellectual property owned by Licensor relating to *Making Cinderella*, **in all media now known or hereafter devised, throughout the universe, in perpetuity**.

**"Licensed Project"** means the documentary program and associated commercial activity concerning the men's basketball program of **Davidson College** for the **[2027–28]** season, and reasonable follow-on content in respect of that program during the Term.

**"Marks"** means the trademarks, service marks, logos, and trade dress of *Making Cinderella* and Cinderella Corp., whether registered or unregistered.

**"Term"** has the meaning in Section 6.

**2.1 Grant.** Licensor grants Licensee a **limited, non-transferable, non-sublicensable** (except per Section 2.4) license, during the Term, to use the Format and the Marks **solely to develop, produce, finance, market, distribute, and exploit the Licensed Project.**

**2.2 Field of use.** The license extends **only** to the Licensed Project. It confers no right in respect of any other institution, season, program, territory-specific adaptation, spin-off, or derivative work.

**2.3 Reservation of rights.** **All rights not expressly granted are reserved to Licensor.** Without limiting that reservation, Licensor retains the exclusive right to:
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) license the Format to any other special purpose vehicle, institution, or production, **including concurrently with the Licensed Project**;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) produce, license, or exploit any spin-off, derivative, international adaptation, or format sale;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) exploit the Marks in any manner outside the Licensed Project; and
&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) determine the sequencing, order, and timing of all projects under the Franchise.

**2.4 Distributor sublicense.** Licensee may grant a distributor a sublicense to use the Marks **solely as necessary to distribute and promote the Licensed Project**, on terms no broader than this Agreement and subject to Licensor's prior written approval, not to be unreasonably withheld.

**2.5 No ownership.** Licensee acquires **no ownership interest** in the Format, the Marks, or any Franchise IP. All goodwill arising from Licensee's use of the Marks **inures solely to Licensor**.

## 3. OWNERSHIP AND NEW MATERIALS

**3.1 Licensor ownership.** As between the parties, Licensor is and remains the sole and exclusive owner of the Format, the Marks, and all Franchise IP.

**3.2 Improvements assigned.** Any improvement, adaptation, refinement, or derivative of the Format conceived or developed by Licensee, or by any producer, contractor, or third party engaged by Licensee, in connection with the Licensed Project **is hereby assigned to Licensor**, and Licensee shall procure equivalent assignments in each of its downstream agreements.

**3.3 Downstream flow-through.** Licensee shall include in every agreement with a production company, distributor, talent, sponsor, or institution provisions consistent with Sections 2.5, 3.1 and 3.2, and shall not enter into any agreement inconsistent with this Agreement. **[This is the clause that prevents a production partner from acquiring format rights through a side door.]**

**3.4 Program copyright.** Copyright in the finished episodes of the Licensed Project shall vest as agreed between Licensee and its distributor, **provided** that in no event shall any such arrangement transfer, encumber, or impair the Format, the Marks, or any Franchise IP.

**6.1 Term.** From the Effective Date until the earlier of (a) **[five (5)] years**, coterminous with the life of Licensee, or (b) termination under this Section.

### Why this document matters (internal)
Every counterparty on the Davidson project — Curry, Unanimous, Davidson College, the capital partner, the production company, the distributor, the athletes — contracts with **the SPV**. The SPV only ever holds a **limited, revocable, single-project license** to the Format. Consequences:
1. **No counterparty can acquire the franchise**, because the entity they deal with never owned it.
2. **§3.3 closes the side door** — a production partner cannot pick up format rights through its own agreement with the SPV.
3. **§2.3(d) preserves sequencing** — Licensor decides which project goes first, so no single SPV can claim the launch position.
4. **§6.3 gives clean reversion** — a failed project returns everything and contaminates nothing.
5. **§9.2 blocks change-of-control leakage** — an acquirer of the SPV does not thereby acquire the license.
