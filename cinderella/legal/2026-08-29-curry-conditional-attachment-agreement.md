# TERM SHEET — DAVIDSON PROJECT
### Talent Attachment · *Making Cinderella*

This Term Sheet (this "**Term Sheet**") is entered into as of **[____], 2026** (the "**Effective Date**") by and among:

- **Cinderella Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Cinderella**" or the "**Company**");
- **[Davidson SPV Name], LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company formed to develop, finance, and produce the Davidson Project (the "**SPV**"); and
- **Unanimous Media, LLC** ("**Unanimous**"), on behalf of and together with **Stephen Curry** ("**Talent**").

---

## RECITALS

**A.** Cinderella owns the *Making Cinderella* format, brand, and franchise intellectual property, under which private capital funds a Division I basketball program and the resulting season is captured as a premium documentary series.

**B.** The parties wish to document the terms on which Talent will attach to **one specific project within that franchise — the Davidson Project** — while providing each party the protections it requires before obligations become binding.

**C.** The parties acknowledge that (i) Talent requires confirmation that financing for the Davidson Project is real and committed, and (ii) the prospective capital partner requires confirmation of Talent's genuine attachment and commitment of time. **This Term Sheet is structured to satisfy both conditions simultaneously and to eliminate the circularity that has delayed the Davidson Project.**

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**"Capital Partner"** means the institutional or private capital source funding the SPV, initially anticipated to be **[The Chernin Group]** or such other source as Cinderella designates.

**"Confirmation Meeting"** means a meeting or videoconference of not less than **[sixty (60)] minutes** attended by **Talent personally**, a senior representative of the Capital Partner, and Cinderella, for the purpose of the Capital Partner satisfying itself as to Talent's attachment and commitment.

**"Davidson Project"** means the documentary program and associated commercial activity concerning the men's basketball program of **Davidson College** for the **[2027–28]** season, developed under the *Making Cinderella* franchise and financed through the SPV.

**"Financing Confirmation"** means written confirmation, delivered to Unanimous, that the SPV has received binding commitments for not less than **$[____]** of the Davidson Project budget.

**"Franchise"** means the *Making Cinderella* format, brand, and all other projects, schools, seasons, and special purpose vehicles thereunder, **other than the Davidson Project**.

**"Outside Date"** means **[December 31, 2026]**.

**"Trigger Date"** means the date on which both (a) the Confirmation Meeting has occurred and (b) Financing Confirmation has been delivered.

---

## 2. BINDING EFFECT — THE CORE MECHANIC

**2.0 Intent to be bound.** Notwithstanding the caption "Term Sheet," **the parties intend the provisions identified in Section 2.1 to be legally binding and enforceable upon execution**, and the remainder to become legally binding and enforceable upon the Trigger Date. This Term Sheet is **not** a letter of intent, an agreement to agree, or a statement of non-binding intent.

**2.1 Provisions binding on execution.** The following bind each party immediately upon execution: **Section 2** (Binding Effect), **Section 2.2** (Mutual Obligations Prior to the Trigger Date), **Section 9** (Confidentiality), **Section 10** (Non-Circumvention), and **Section 12** (General). All other provisions — including Sections 3 through 8 and 11 — **become binding and effective only upon the Trigger Date.**

**2.2 Mutual Obligations Prior to the Trigger Date.** Between the Effective Date and the Outside Date:

&nbsp;&nbsp;**(a) Talent / Unanimous** shall make Talent available, on reasonable notice and at a mutually convenient time, to attend **one Confirmation Meeting**; and shall not, during such period, attach to any Competing Project as defined in Section 8.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**(b) Cinderella** shall use commercially reasonable efforts to (i) convene the Confirmation Meeting and (ii) obtain and deliver Financing Confirmation.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**(c)** Cinderella may present this Term Sheet, and Talent's conditional attachment to the Davidson Project, to prospective distribution partners, capital partners, sponsors, and institutions — **provided** that any such presentation accurately describes the attachment as **conditional** and subject to the terms hereof.

**2.3 Automatic Termination — Mutual.** If the Trigger Date has not occurred on or before the **Outside Date**, this Term Sheet **terminates automatically and in its entirety**, without liability to any party, and each party is released from all obligations hereunder. Neither party bears the risk alone: **Talent is not bound if the money never confirms, and the Capital Partner is not bound if Talent never confirms.**

**2.4 Extension.** The Outside Date may be extended once, by up to **[ninety (90)] days**, by mutual written agreement.

**2.5 No Obligation to Proceed.** Nothing in this Term Sheet obligates the Capital Partner to fund, or Cinderella to proceed with, the Davidson Project.

---

## 3. SCOPE — THE DAVIDSON PROJECT ONLY

**3.1 Attachment is project-specific.** Talent attaches solely to the **Davidson Project**. This Term Sheet does not attach Talent to, and confers no rights or obligations with respect to, the **Franchise** or any other project, school, season, or special purpose vehicle.

**3.2 No franchise or format rights.** Talent and Unanimous acquire no right, title, or interest in the *Making Cinderella* format, brand, trademarks, or franchise intellectual property, all of which are and remain the exclusive property of Cinderella Corp.

**3.3 Cinderella's freedom to operate.** Nothing in this Term Sheet restricts Cinderella from developing, financing, producing, or exploiting any other project under the Franchise, with any other talent, at any other institution, **including concurrently with the Davidson Project**, and including projects that may be presented to or ordered by a distribution partner before the Davidson Project.

**3.4 Sequencing.** The Davidson Project is not designated as the first season, launch title, or any particular position within the Franchise, and Cinderella retains sole discretion over the sequencing of Franchise projects.

> **Drafting note (internal):** §3 is the provision that makes Curry an anchor rather than a cage. Attaching to "the Project," "the Series," or "Season 1" would break if a distributor wished to launch elsewhere. Attaching to **the Davidson Project** does not.

---

## 4. SERVICES

**4.1 Commitment.** From and after the Trigger Date, Talent shall render, over a **[twelve (12)]-month** period:

&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **[four (4) to six (6)] days** of in-person services, to include at least **[one]** campus visit and attendance at **[two]** games; and
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) **[twelve (12)] hours** of remote on-camera services (interviews, videoconferences with coaching staff, and similar).

**4.2 Scheduling.** All services shall be scheduled on reasonable advance notice, subject to Talent's professional and NBA obligations, which take precedence.

**4.3 Nature of services.** Services comprise on-camera participation, use of name, image, and likeness in connection with the Davidson Project, and reasonable promotional and sponsor-activation activity. **Talent has no obligation to render services in connection with any other Franchise project.**

**4.4 Approvals.** Talent shall have reasonable consultation rights over the portrayal of Talent and, where practicable, **[approval over the use of Talent's name and likeness in marketing materials]**. **[Confirm scope.]**

## 5. COMPENSATION

**5.1 Equity.** Talent (or Unanimous, as directed) shall receive **[thirty percent (30%)]** of the equity of the **SPV**, subject to the SPV's operating agreement. This is an interest in the **Davidson SPV only** and confers no interest in Cinderella Corp. or any other SPV.

**5.2 Cash.** Talent shall receive a cash fee of **$[____]**, payable **[on the Trigger Date / in installments across the service period]**.

**5.3 No compensation prior to the Trigger Date.** No fee, equity, or other consideration is earned or payable unless and until the Trigger Date occurs.

**5.4 Expenses.** The SPV shall pay Talent's reasonable travel and accommodation expenses for in-person services, at a **[standard to be agreed]** level.

## 6. CREDIT

**6.1 Talent** shall receive an **Executive Producer** credit on the Davidson Project, in a position and size to be agreed, **[subject to distributor approval]**.

**6.2 Unanimous** shall receive **[a production credit — scope TBD]**. **[See internal note.]**

**6.3 Norman C. de Silva** shall receive a **"Created by"** credit in **first position** on the Davidson Project and on every Franchise project.

> **Drafting note (internal):** Unanimous is a production company and may seek a producing role on the Davidson Project. That is negotiable **provided** it is confined to the Davidson Project, carries no Franchise or format rights, and does not disturb §3 or §7. Do not concede a Franchise-level producing position.

---

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1** Cinderella Corp. owns and retains all right, title, and interest in the *Making Cinderella* format, brand, trademarks, and franchise intellectual property, and all derivative works, in perpetuity.

**7.2** Talent retains all rights in Talent's own name, image, likeness, and personal brand, and grants the SPV a license to use them **solely in connection with the Davidson Project** and its promotion, for the term and territories to be specified in the long-form agreement.

**7.3** Nothing herein grants Talent, Unanimous, or any affiliate any right to produce, license, or exploit any Franchise project other than the Davidson Project.

---

## 8. EXCLUSIVITY

**8.1 Competing Project.** From the Effective Date until the earlier of (a) the Outside Date, if the Trigger Date has not occurred, or (b) **[eighteen (18)] months** following the Trigger Date, Talent shall not attach to, or render on-camera services for, any documentary program concerning **a Division I men's college basketball program in which Talent or an affiliate holds a financing or ownership interest** (a "**Competing Project**").

**8.2 Carve-outs.** Section 8.1 does not restrict Talent's ordinary professional activities, including NBA-related media, Unanimous's unrelated productions, brand and endorsement work, journalism or interviews, or documentary participation concerning Talent's own career or the Warriors.

> **Drafting note (internal):** exclusivity is deliberately narrow. A broad "college basketball" restriction would be resisted by Unanimous and is unnecessary — the goal is only to prevent a directly competing *Making Cinderella*-style project, not to constrain Curry's career.

---

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall hold the terms of this Term Sheet and all non-public information of the others in confidence, subject to disclosure to professional advisors, prospective capital partners, distributors, and institutions on a need-to-know basis under equivalent obligations, or as required by law. **Binding on execution.**

---

## 10. NON-CIRCUMVENTION

For **[twenty-four (24)] months** following the Effective Date, no party shall circumvent any other party with respect to Davidson College, the Capital Partner, or any distributor, sponsor, or counterparty introduced in connection with the Davidson Project. **Binding on execution.**

---

## 11. LONG-FORM AGREEMENT

The parties shall negotiate in good faith and execute a long-form agreement consistent with these terms within **[forty-five (45)] days** following the Trigger Date. **Until superseded, this Term Sheet governs and, from the Trigger Date, is binding and enforceable in accordance with its terms.**
