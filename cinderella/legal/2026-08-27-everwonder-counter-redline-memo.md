# EverWonder Shopping Agreement — Counter-Proposal & Redline Memo

**Document reviewed:** "Making Cinderella — EWS Shopping Agreement," Term Sheet + Exhibit A (Standard Terms & Conditions), 89 Blocks Holdings LLC d/b/a EverWonder Studio ("EWS") / Cinderella Corp. ("Producer"), received 2026-08-26.

---

## 1. Summary position

The Term Sheet is framed as a shopping agreement. **Exhibit A ¶2 operates as an outright, perpetual, worldwide assignment of the Project — including copyrights, trademarks, derivatives, merchandising, and ancillary rights — to EWS, on a work-made-for-hire basis.** Combined with a reversion clause that returns nothing, an exclusivity covenant broad enough to capture the franchise, a nine-month tail that sweeps in Producer-originated buyers, and the absence of any Producer termination or injunctive remedy, the Agreement as drafted transfers ownership and control of the franchise to EWS in exchange for a non-binding effort to sell it.

Cinderella Corp.'s ownership of the *Making Cinderella* format, brand, and franchise IP is not available for assignment. **Items 1 and 2 below are threshold conditions.** If they cannot be agreed in principle, there is no transaction to negotiate.

The balance of this memo sets out the changes required.

---

## 2. Threshold items (no deal without these)

### Item 1 — Ownership: Producer retains all IP; EWS receives a limited shopping license
**Current:** Ex. A ¶2 — EWS owns "all right, title and interest in and to the Project… including… all copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and other intellectual property rights," as a work made for hire, "throughout the universe… in perpetuity," including "all derivative works and adaptations."

**Required:**
- Delete the work-made-for-hire and assignment constructs in their entirety.
- **Cinderella Corp. is and remains the sole owner** of the Project, the *Making Cinderella* format and franchise, all trademarks and brand assets, all derivative and ancillary rights, and all underlying materials.
- EWS receives a **limited, non-exclusive-as-to-ownership, revocable license, for the Shopping Period only, solely to package, market, and present the Project to prospective Buyers.**
- Any transfer of rights to a Buyer occurs **only under a definitive Buyer Agreement executed by Cinderella Corp. as the rights holder**, on terms Cinderella Corp. approves.
- Chain of title is satisfied by Cinderella Corp.'s ownership plus a customary license to the Buyer — not by assignment to EWS.
- Producer's moral-rights waiver is deleted or narrowed to the specific production only.

### Item 2 — Reversion: all rights and materials return to Producer on expiry
**Current:** Term Sheet, *Reversion* — EWS "will own materials created during the Shopping Period," with Producer left "not in a worse position than a member of the general public."

**Required:**
- On expiry or termination for any reason, **all rights revert automatically and immediately to Cinderella Corp.**, and EWS retains nothing.
- **Delete the "general public" formulation** in full. It is not a reversion provision.
- Development materials (deck, sizzle) created by EWS: Producer receives a perpetual license to use them, or a right to acquire them at documented cost. *(Fallback: EWS may retain its raw footage, but Producer retains the underlying Project and all franchise rights unencumbered.)*

## 3. Material items (must be resolved)

### Item 3 — Exclusivity must not encumber the franchise
**Current:** Producer, NDS, and SM may not "develop, produce, or attach to another production that is substantially similar to the Project," during the Shopping Period **and throughout the term of any Buyer agreement** — where the Project is defined as "an audiovisual program about the changing landscape of college sports."

**Required:**
- Exclusivity is limited to **the specific program covering the specific school and season** contemplated by the Buyer Agreement.
- Express carve-out: nothing restricts Cinderella Corp., NDS, or SM from developing, financing, producing, or exploiting **the Making Cinderella franchise, any other season, any other school, any other SPV, or any derivative or spinoff**, whether or not "substantially similar."
- Delete "substantially similar" as the operative standard; substitute the specific Project as defined by school and season.

### Item 4 — Tail Period: narrow it and carve out existing relationships
**Current:** Nine months; captures any third party contacted about the Project during the Shopping Period, expressly including contacts "initiated by… Producer, NDS, SM, or such third party."

**Required:**
- Tail reduced to **three (3) months**.
- Applies **only to Buyers first introduced to the Project by EWS**, evidenced by a written introduction log maintained contemporaneously.
- **Express exclusion of all Producer-originated relationships**, including without limitation: **Netflix, Apple, HBO/Max (WBD), Amazon, Paramount/Skydance, Disney/ESPN**, and any party contacted by Producer prior to the Effective Date. Schedule of excluded parties to be attached and updated at signature.
- Tail entitles EWS to its production fee on a captured deal only — not to credits, approvals, or other rights.

### Item 5 — Ownership and control of the Project's creative direction
**Current:** "Company shall have approval with respect to all key creative decisions… subject to meaningful consultation with Producer."

**Required:**
- **Mutual approval** over key creative decisions, with a good-faith consultation obligation on both sides.
- Producer holds **final approval** over: the *Making Cinderella* brand and its use; any representation of Cinderella Corp.'s business model, capital structure, or investors; and any depiction of participating institutions, athletes, or capital partners that could create NCAA, NIL, or regulatory exposure.
- Deadlock resolution mechanism to be agreed.

### Item 6 — Remove personal liability; align remedies
**Current:** Inducement makes NDS and SM "primarily, jointly and severally liable." Ex. A ¶6 limits Producer to damages and bars termination, rescission, and injunctive relief. Ex. A ¶10 permits termination by Company only, with all granted rights surviving.

**Required:**
- **Delete the Inducement**, or limit individual undertakings to a performance covenant with no financial liability; all monetary obligations rest with Cinderella Corp.
- **Delete ¶6** (no-injunctive-relief). Both parties retain full equitable remedies.
- Termination rights are **mutual**, on equivalent notice and cure.
- On termination for any reason, **the shopping license terminates and all rights revert to Producer.** The current "all rights granted to Company shall survive" language is deleted.
- Producer's indemnity is limited to materials Producer actually supplies, is mutual in scope, and is capped at amounts actually received by Producer under the Agreement.

### Item 7 — Conditions Precedent and cost allocation
**Current:** Producer must attach Unanimous Content, Stephen Curry, and Davidson College at "Producer's sole cost and expense," in form "satisfactory to Company," granting all rights in all media — while ¶8 provides EWS "is not obligated in any manner… including… to enter into an agreement with a Buyer."

**Required:**
- Attachments are delivered in a form reasonably satisfactory to EWS, judged on a **commercially reasonable** standard, and grant only the rights necessary for the specific Project.
- **EWS undertakes affirmative shopping obligations**: a minimum number of Buyer presentations, delivery of the deck and sizzle by a date certain, and regular written status reporting. A non-binding effort clause paired with total rights assignment is not acceptable.
- Producer bears no obligation to attach talent on terms that assign underlying franchise rights to EWS.

### Item 8 — Morals / Conduct Violation
**Current:** Immediate termination for conduct that "in Company's judgement" constitutes moral turpitude or "shocks, insults, or offends a significant portion of the community," with rights surviving.

**Required:**
- Replace the subjective standard with an **objective one**: conviction of a felony, or a plea thereto.
- Expressly provide that **editorial or journalistic content of the Project — including critical examination of the NCAA, collegiate athletics governance, conferences, or their commercial practices — does not constitute a Conduct Violation.**
- On any termination, rights revert to Producer (per Item 6).

## 4. Economic items

### Item 9 — Fees and contingent compensation
**Current:** EWS receives an "industry standard Production Company Fee." NDS and SM receive an EP fee and contingent compensation "to be negotiated in good faith… subject to Buyer approval," with "mutually approved third parties taken off the top."

**Required:**
- **Floor amounts** for NDS and SM EP fees, stated in dollars or as a defined percentage of the production budget, payable per season.
- **Defined contingent compensation** — a stated percentage of a defined pool, with the definition of the pool and any off-the-top deductions specified and mutually approved in advance.
- Producer's compensation is not subject to EWS approval; Buyer approval only where genuinely required by the Buyer Agreement.
- EWS's Production Company Fee is a **fee for services only** — no backend participation, no equity, no ownership, consistent with Item 1.
- Development costs advanced by EWS are recouped **from the production budget only**, and are not recoupable against Producer's fees or against Cinderella Corp. if no sale occurs.

### Item 10 — Credits
**Current:** EWS receives four EP credits in first position; NDS and SM receive one EP credit each on a shared card, subject to Buyer approval.

**Required:**
- **NDS receives a separate card, first position** as creator/executive producer of the format.
- SM receives an EP credit.
- **EWS credits reduced to two (2)**, positioned after Producer's.
- A **"Making Cinderella" / Cinderella Corp. presentation or production credit** on all episodes and materials.
- Credit obligations bind EWS and any Buyer to the extent EWS can secure them.

### Item 11 — Lock
**Current:** Mutual lock, "subject to Buyer approval," covering "any and all derivatives."

**Required:**
- Lock is mutual and, as to NDS, is **not subject to Buyer approval** for the initial season. Producer's principal is not removable from the show he created.
- Derivatives are governed by Item 1 — they belong to Cinderella Corp. and are not swept in by the lock.

### Item 12 — Assignment
**Current:** EWS may assign to any affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets, without consent; Producer may not assign.

**Required:** Assignment rights are **mutual and reciprocal**, and any assignment by EWS of the shopping license requires Producer's prior written consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 5. Acceptable as drafted

For completeness, the following are acceptable and should be preserved: the twelve-month Shopping Period with a six-month active-negotiation extension; EWS funding development costs; EWS serving as production services company of record; the mutual confidentiality provision (Ex. A ¶9); the force-majeure construct (Ex. A ¶5); and the reasonableness covenant (Ex. A ¶17).

## 6. Negotiating notes (internal — not for transmission)

**Leverage.** Cinderella Corp. holds the assets EWS needs and does not have: the format, the Curry/Davidson attachment, the school pipeline, and direct, live conversations with **Netflix, Apple, HBO, Amazon, Paramount and Disney/ESPN**. EWS is a credible mid-tier shop with a short track record and no franchise-defining sports title. The terms proposed would be aggressive from a top-tier producer; from EWS they are not supportable.

**Alternatives are real and should be developed in parallel.** Boardwalk Pictures (Wrexham, Last Chance U, Cheer), Words + Pictures (Connor Schell — direct line exists), and Religion of Sports are each ranked materially above EWS and are natural homes for this format. Producer should open at least one alternative conversation before responding, and should be willing to proceed without EWS.

**Sequencing.** Transmit Items 1 and 2 first, as threshold conditions, before negotiating the balance. EWS's response on Item 1 determines whether the remaining items are worth negotiating.

**Walk-away posture.** Producer's position is that a shopping arrangement is welcome and a production partnership is available — ownership of the franchise is not. That position should be stated plainly and held.
