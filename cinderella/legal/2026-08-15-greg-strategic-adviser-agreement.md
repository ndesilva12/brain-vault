# STRATEGIC ADVISER AGREEMENT

This Strategic Adviser Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of **[____], 2026** (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Cinderella Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Greg Kristof** ("**Adviser**").

## 1. Services
1.1 **Role.** Adviser will serve as a **non-exclusive Strategic Adviser**, providing **strategic guidance and network access** — including his basketball roster/NIL/coach/event network and general strategic input on the Company's playbook. Advisory and introductions only.
1.2 **Limits.** Adviser is **not an officer, director, employee, or agent**, has **no authority to bind** the Company, and will **not execute, negotiate to close, or sign** agreements on the Company's behalf. Deal execution rests with the Company.
1.3 **Non-exclusive.** Adviser may continue his own businesses (including Zero Gravity / 6th Sense) provided he complies with Sections 5–7.
1.4 **Time.** No minimum hours; Adviser will make reasonable efforts to be available for periodic consultation.

## 3. Independent Contractor
Adviser is an independent contractor, responsible for his own taxes, and is not entitled to employee benefits. Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.
