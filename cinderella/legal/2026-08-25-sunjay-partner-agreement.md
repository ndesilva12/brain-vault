## 2. Base Equity — [15]%
2.1 **Grant.** The Company grants Partner restricted **Class A Common Stock** equal to **[fifteen percent (15%)]** of the Company (the "**Base Shares**"), measured on the **fully-diluted capitalization of the Company immediately PRIOR to the Seed Round** (i.e., **pre-money / pre-Seed**).
2.2 **Seed dilution applies.** Because the Base Shares are measured **pre-Seed**, Partner **is diluted by the Seed Round** on the same basis as the Founders. For illustration only, a **[15]%** pre-Seed position becomes approximately **[12.75]%** following a **[15]%** Seed Round. Partner has **no anti-dilution protection** with respect to the Seed Round or any subsequent issuance.
2.3 **Vesting (cliff schedule).** The Base Shares vest in three tranches:
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **[5]%** — **fully vested on the Effective Date**;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) **[5]%** — vests on the **[first]** anniversary of the Effective Date; and
&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) **[5]%** — vests on the **[second]** anniversary of the Effective Date,
&nbsp;&nbsp;in each case for (b) and (c) **subject to Partner's continuous service** through the applicable vesting date. **These are cliffs, not monthly vesting** — no partial credit accrues between anniversaries. **[Confirm; monthly vesting after the initial tranche is the alternative if you want to soften the all-or-nothing effect at each anniversary.]**
2.4 **Forfeiture.** On termination of service for any reason, all **unvested** Base Shares are immediately forfeited to (or repurchasable by) the Company at the lower of cost or fair market value. Vested shares remain subject to the Stockholders' Agreement.
2.5 **Dilution.** The Base Shares are subject to **ordinary, pro-rata dilution** on all issuances. Partner has **no anti-dilution protection.**

## 3. Milestone Equity — up to an additional [5]% (to a [20]% maximum)

3.2 **Outside Date.** The Milestone Shares must be fully earned on or before **[December 31, 2029 — confirm]** (the "**Outside Date**"). Any portion not earned by the Outside Date is permanently forfeited.
3.3 **All-or-nothing.** Each Track is **binary**. Partial, preliminary, or substantially-completed performance earns **nothing**. No pro-rata or partial credit is available under either Track.
