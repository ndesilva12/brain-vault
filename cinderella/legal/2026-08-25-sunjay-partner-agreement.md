## 2. BASE EQUITY — 20%

2.1 **Grant.** The Company grants Partner restricted **Class A Common Stock** equal to **twenty percent (20%)** of the Company (the "**Base Shares**"), measured on the **fully-diluted capitalization of the Company immediately PRIOR to the Seed Round** (i.e., **pre-money / pre-Seed**).

2.2 **Seed dilution applies.** Because the Base Shares are measured **pre-Seed**, Partner **is diluted by the Seed Round** on the same basis as Norman and Greg Kristof. **[MATERIAL — CONFIRM AGAINST THE FINAL CAP TABLE.]** For illustration only: on a pre-Seed capitalization of Norman 72% / Partner 20% / Ankur Jain 4% / Greg Kristof 4%, and assuming a **[15]%** Seed Round in which **Ankur Jain's position does not dilute** (per the Company's separate arrangement with him), Norman, Partner, and Greg collectively absorb more than 15% dilution to hold Ankur flat and still deliver 15% to the Seed investors. This figure is illustrative only and must be confirmed against the actual Seed Round terms and cap table model before use. Partner has **no anti-dilution protection** with respect to the Seed Round or any subsequent issuance.

2.3 **Vesting (cliff schedule).** The Base Shares vest in four tranches:
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **[5]%** — **fully vested on the Effective Date**;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) **[5]%** — vests on the **first** anniversary of the Effective Date;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) **[5]%** — vests on the **second** anniversary of the Effective Date; and
&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) **[5]%** — vests on the **third** anniversary of the Effective Date,
in each case for (b), (c) and (d) **subject to Partner's continuous service** through the applicable vesting date. **These are cliffs, not monthly vesting** — no partial credit accrues between anniversaries.

2.4 **Forfeiture.** On termination of service for any reason, all **unvested** Base Shares are immediately forfeited to (or repurchasable by) the Company at the lower of cost or fair market value. Vested shares remain subject to the Stockholders' Agreement.

2.5 **Dilution.** The Base Shares are subject to **ordinary, pro-rata dilution** on all issuances (subject to Section 2.2 as to the Seed Round). Partner has **no anti-dilution protection.**

## 3. MILESTONE EQUITY — UP TO AN ADDITIONAL 5% (TO A 25% MAXIMUM)

3.1 **The Milestone Award.** Partner may earn **up to an additional [five percent (5%)]** of the Company in Class A Common Stock (the "**Milestone Shares**"), bringing Partner's maximum equity to **[twenty-five percent (25%)]**, by achieving **Track A and/or Track B** below. **Each Track, if achieved, earns [two and one-half percent (2.5%)], and the Tracks stack** — achieving both earns **[5]%** in the aggregate. **Each Track may be earned once only**; a second qualifying event under a Track already earned earns nothing further.

3.2 **Outside Date.** Each Track must be fully earned on or before **[December 31, 2029 — confirm]** (the "**Outside Date**"). Any Track not fully earned by the Outside Date is permanently forfeited.

3.3 **All-or-nothing; Tracks independent.** Each Track is **binary**. Partial or preliminary performance earns **nothing**. The Tracks are independent of one another: achievement, failure, or clawback of one Track does not affect Milestone Shares properly earned under the other.

### TRACK A — Lead or Presenting Sponsor, Any Season

The Milestone is earned if:
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(a) Sponsor.** The sponsor is one of the brands listed on **Schedule A** (illustrative examples only) or **any other brand approved by the Board**, in its sole discretion.
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(b) Status.** The sponsor holds **Lead Sponsor or Presenting Sponsor** status for **any single season** of any Company school project, or the **Board otherwise determines**, in its sole discretion, that the sponsor is the **most prominent sponsor of that season**. The sponsorship need not cover more than one school or more than one season, and **no minimum dollar amount is required.**
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(c) Definitive agreement.** A **definitive, fully executed** sponsorship agreement is in place (a term sheet, LOI, or non-binding commitment does **not** qualify).
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(d) Procuring cause.** Partner is the **direct procuring cause** of the sponsorship, as determined by the Board in its sole discretion. Deals sourced through Norman, existing Company relationships, retained agencies, or third-party finders do **not** qualify.
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(e) Arm's length.** The sponsor is not an affiliate of, and has no material pre-existing economic relationship with, Partner or his affiliates.
&nbsp;&nbsp;**(f) Sustained performance.** The agreement remains in effect, and has not been terminated or materially reduced other than due to Company breach, for **[twelve (12)] months** following execution. **Clawback:** if it is terminated or materially reduced within that period, the Milestone Shares attributable to Track A are forfeited (or repurchasable at cost), whether or not previously issued. Forfeiture under this paragraph does not affect any Milestone Shares earned under Track B.
