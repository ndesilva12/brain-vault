## 1. DUAL-CLASS SUPER-VOTING  (in the A&R Certificate, Article FOURTH)

**Terms to adopt:**
- Authorize **two classes of common stock**, identical in every economic respect
  (dividends, liquidation, per-share treatment) — **only votes differ**:
  - **Class A Common** — 1 vote/share (advisors, investors, option holders)
  - **Class B Common** — **10 votes/share** (Norman only) **[DECIDE: 10:1 is standard
    and more than enough — with 3M Class B = 30M votes, you keep voting majority
    against any Class A issuable under the 10M cap]**
- **Reclassify** Norman's 3,000,000 Common → 3,000,000 **Class B**.
- All new issuances (advisors, round, options) are **Class A**.
- **Auto-conversion of Class B → Class A** upon:
  (a) any transfer other than to Norman's revocable trust / estate-planning vehicle he controls;
  (b) **[DECIDE]** Norman ceasing to be a director or employee of the Company (recommended — ties super-votes to active involvement);
  (c) **[DECIDE]** Norman's death (convert = no dynastic control; retain = estate keeps control — most founders convert).
- **Class B protective vote:** no amendment may adversely alter Class B rights
  without the affirmative vote of a majority of Class B (protects the super-vote itself).

**Control math (why this works):** 3M Class B × 10 = 30M votes. Post-round Class A
≈ 1.8M shares = 1.8M votes → your voting power ≈ **94%** on ~62.5% economics. You
retain a voting majority until Class A outstanding exceeds 30M shares — impossible
under the current 10M authorized cap without a further amendment you'd control.

**Execute:** assemble into the A&R (⚠️ platform/lawyer for the actual charter),
approve by board + sole-stockholder consent, file with Delaware, reissue as Class B.

## 4. PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS  (two sides — keep investors' NARROW, add founder's)

### 4a. Investor protective provisions — keep NARROW
If **SAFE round (recommended for DIY):** SAFEs carry **almost no** protective
provisions — this is the simplest way to keep investor control minimal. Prefer this.

If **priced round**, limit investor-consent items to genuine fundamentals ONLY:
- ✅ Adverse amendments to investors' own class rights
- ✅ Creating stock senior to theirs
- ✅ Voluntary dissolution / assignment for benefit of creditors
- ❌ **Do NOT concede consent over:** hiring/firing, salaries, budgets, new financings
  generally, the business plan, school/celebrity selection, day-to-day operations,
  board expansion, or issuing junior/ordinary stock. These are where investors
  quietly take control — refuse them at seed.

### 4b. Founder protective provisions — ADD these (Voting Agreement / charter)
Actions requiring **Norman's** consent (or a Class-B/supermajority he controls):
- Removing Norman as CEO/President
- Changing the fundamental business or the franchise format
- Selling/merging the Company or licensing the core IP
- Amending the dual-class structure or the Voting Agreement
- Increasing the board size or authorized shares
