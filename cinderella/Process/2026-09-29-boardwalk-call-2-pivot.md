# Boardwalk call #2 — they want in, and they want to change the thesis

_2026-09-29. Andrew Fried + CFO **Eric Johnson** + COO **Dane Liligard**. The most consequential
call the project has had._

---

## What they said

1. ⭐ **They want to produce it**, and will **finance their own side in exchange for an equity
   position in the SPV** — explicitly framed as maximising alignment with us.
2. ⭐⭐ **They want to rethink the celebrity premise.** Because the format is *real-time*, a
   traditional name who flies in for a few games and waves is worth far less than **someone who
   posts daily and builds engagement as it happens** rather than lending retroactive glamour.
3. **The "GameStop" framing** — one unknown team becomes *the team of the people*, driven by a group
   of influencers/celebrities **on the ground and engaged daily.** Fried likes **Merrimack**.
4. **Floated crowdfunding part of the SPV** so fans hold a stake in the team's success.
5. **He is making calls now:** Jake & Logan Paul (manager first), Jesser, and TCG alternatives
   including **Shamrock Capital** — which is ⭐ **Boardwalk's own minority investor**
   (`2026-09-27-captive-producer-pattern.md`), so that is a warm intra-cap-table path, not a cold one.
6. He wants **a list of names from us**, plus he will bring back feedback from those first calls.
   **Regroup in a few days.**

## Why this matters beyond the obvious

- **Equity in the SPV instead of a fee** is a real alignment signal and a departure from the
  EverWonder posture (which demanded the **format**). ⚠️ **SPV equity is not format equity** — that
  distinction is the whole ballgame and must be held. Cinderella Corp keeps the Format License.
- **Their CFO and COO on a second call** means this is being resourced internally, not admired.
- ⭐ **The thesis shift is probably correct, and it solves our hardest problem.** Every celebrity
  thread has stalled on the same thing — a big name's time. **Creators have no such constraint:
  posting daily *is* their job.** Availability stops being the binding constraint.
- It also **reframes the Curry standoff**. If the format does not require a megastar, the
  Curry/Unanimous/TCG circularity stops being on the critical path.
