bank — with boutique advisory **M. Klein & Company**. ~60 people across **New York, LA and London**.

Services: **IPOs, fund and capital formation, strategic access to institutional capital, M&A
(including large-cap media), sports team M&A, capital markets, media-rights advisory** — and
**principal investing**, not only advisory.

⭐ Its **sports media advisory practice, led by President Alan Gold, has advised leagues,
federations, CONFERENCES and teams.** They work the college-media-rights market we operate inside.

## The read

**They are sizing a transaction.** Two bankers plus a banking assistant in a first real meeting is
a deal team. The question they are answering internally is *which desk owns this and is there a fee
in it.*

**Three things they could want, and they are not equally good for us:**

1. **Sponsorship / naming rights mandate** (DeAngelis) — best fit. CAA is genuinely best-in-class
   here and non-endemic brands are exactly our need.
   ⚠️ **But this sits on top of Sunjay's Partner Agreement Track A milestone** (lead/presenting
   sponsor, no dollar minimum). Settle internally whether a CAA-sourced sponsor triggers it
   **before** the call.
2. **Celebrity supply from the basketball roster** (Frischer) — genuinely valuable and a new lane.
   ⚠️ Costs talent equity, which currently opens at **10–15%**.
3. **Capital advisory / principal investment** (Evolution) — the one to slow down.
   ⚠️ Conflicts with **Ankur's raise track**, and sits across from **RedBird and TCG**.

**⚠️ The conflict question nobody has asked yet:** CAA Evolution advises **conferences**. Do they
advise the A-10, or anyone touching Davidson or St. Joseph's? If so, that is both the most useful
door on the board and a circumvention exposure. **Ask directly.**
