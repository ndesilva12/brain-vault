## Rationale notes for investor-facing version

**Closing bonus framing:** A $1.5M friends-and-family raise run through a placement agent typically costs 5–10% ($75K–$150K) in fees. Norman is running the raise himself. The $60K (4%) closing bonus — paid only on successful close — is a discount to that alternative, not a founder perk. Frame it as pay-for-performance.

**Founder comp framing:** Combined Norman comp ($260K, 17.3% of raise) is on the high side for a raise this size and will draw questions from sophisticated checks. The defense: this isn't a product company where capital goes to engineering — the product is Norman's NBA scouting network, agent relationships, and franchise IP architecture. People costs at 40% total (not just founder) is reasonable for a relationship-driven business, but the rationale should be in the deck next to the number, not left for an investor to ask about.

**Open item:** Securities counsel (Loeb & Loeb) needs to bless the raise mechanics — Reg D exemption, accredited investor verification, subscription docs — before solicitation of the 15 checks begins. Normally this work predates the proceeds that would fund it; worth asking whether Loeb & Loeb will defer billing against close.
