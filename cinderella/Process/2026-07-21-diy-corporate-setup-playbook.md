# DIY Corporate Setup Playbook — Pre-Seed (before formal Loeb engagement)
_Session: 2026-07-21 · NOT legal advice — an organized action plan. Items marked
⚠️ warrant a template platform (Clerky/Carta/Atlas) or flat-fee lawyer review
before you sign/file. Everything else is safe to execute yourself._

**Golden rule of sequence:** do the **dual-class charter amendment while you are
still the SOLE stockholder** (Phase 2), BEFORE issuing any advisor/investor stock
(Phases 3–4). After outside stock exists, changing the charter needs their consent.

---

## PHASE 1 — Cure the corporate chain (DIY · low risk)

**1.1 Incorporator handoff.** Northwest was the actual incorporator. You need their
signed **Action of Incorporator** resigning and appointing you as sole director +
adopting the bylaws. If it exists, file it. If not, request it from Northwest
(standard; they produce these). *Sign:* Northwest signs. *File:* record book.

**1.2 Omnibus ratification.** You (sole director + sole stockholder) sign one
written consent ratifying all prior actions (bylaws, officer appointments, IP
assignment, 3M-share issuance) to cure any defect from the handoff gap. *Sign:*
you, twice (as director and as stockholder). *File:* record book. Draft below.

**1.3 Discard** the unfiled April 14 "secondary" certificate (1A) and the blank
"Initial Resolution Appointments" (1B) — inoperative; don't adopt them.

> DRAFT — OMNIBUS WRITTEN CONSENT (review before signing)
> "The undersigned, being the sole director and sole stockholder of Cinderella
> Corp. (the 'Company'), hereby ratifies, adopts, and approves in all respects each
> action taken on behalf of the Company since incorporation, including without
> limitation: (a) the adoption of the Bylaws; (b) the election of Norman deSilva as
> sole director and appointment as President, Secretary, and Treasurer; (c) the
> Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement dated April 14, 2026; and (d) the
> issuance of 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Norman deSilva under the Founder
> Stock Purchase and Restriction Agreement dated April 14, 2026. Effective [date].
> ___________ Norman deSilva, Sole Director / Sole Stockholder"

## PHASE 2 — ⚠️ Amend the charter: dual-class + exculpation (file with Delaware, while sole stockholder)

**Decide the terms first:**
- Two classes: **Class A** common (1 vote/share, everyone else) and **Class B**
  common (10 votes/share → you). Same economics per share; votes differ.
- **Auto-convert** Class B → Class A on transfer to anyone but you/your trust
  (keeps super-votes with you only).
- Add **§102(b)(7) director exculpation** and indemnification at the charter level
  (fixes the gap — bylaws alone don't provide the monetary-damages shield).
- Reclassify your 3,000,000 Common → 3,000,000 **Class B**.

**Execute:**
1. Prepare the **Amended & Restated Certificate of Incorporation** with the above.
   ⚠️ Don't hand-draft — generate via **Clerky/Carta/Atlas** (they support
   dual-class) or a flat-fee lawyer for this one doc.
2. *Sign:* **Board consent** (you) approving the A&R + reclassification, then
   **Sole Stockholder consent** (you) approving the A&R. (DGCL needs both board
   and stockholder approval for a charter amendment — trivial while you're both.)
3. *File:* the A&R with the **Delaware Division of Corporations** — easiest path is
   to have **Northwest file it for you** (they offer this), or file online/by mail
   yourself. *Cost:* ~$200+ to Delaware (amendment fee; + optional expedite/certified).
4. Issue a **new Class B stock certificate** (or book-entry) for your 3M; update
   the ledger; cancel the old common certificate.

*Do all of Phase 2 BEFORE Phase 3.*

## PHASE 3 — Equity & control docs for the round (DIY-able with templates)

**3.1 Option pool (optional).** If you'll grant options to hires, adopt an **Equity
Incentive Plan** and reserve a pool from authorized-unissued (Class A). *Sign:*
board + stockholder consent. Platform-generated.

**3.2 Advisor stock — Sunjay, Ankur, Greg.** Each gets a **Stock Purchase &
Restriction Agreement** (Class A) **with vesting** (milestone-based fits Sunjay &
Ankur). Mirror your founder terms (repurchase right, ROFR, transfer limits).
*Sign:* company (you) + each advisor. *Each advisor files their OWN 83(b) within 30
days of their grant* — set a reminder; this one you can't miss like the last.

**3.3 Voting Agreement.** All holders (you, advisors, incoming investors) agree to
vote for your board designees and support your control. *Sign:* all holders at
closing. Platform-generated or template.

**3.4 ROFR/Co-Sale (optional, standard).** Company/founder right of first refusal on
transfers. Bundle with the round docs.

## PHASE 4 — ⚠️ The seed round itself (securities law — get help here)

**4.1 Pick the instrument:** priced round (issues Class A now, needs a valuation +
protective provisions) vs **SAFE** (simpler, converts later — easier to DIY). We've
modeled $10M post; a SAFE at a $10M cap is the lighter-weight path.

**4.2 Investor docs:** SAFE or Stock Purchase Agreement + **narrow** protective
provisions. Platform-generated (YC SAFE is free/standard).

**4.3 Accredited-investor check:** 506(b) (no general solicitation, self-certify) is
simplest for friends-and-network checks; 506(c) (public raise) requires verifying
accreditation. Given Ankur/advisors + ~15 known investors, **506(b)** fits.

**4.4 File Form D** with the SEC via EDGAR **within 15 days of the first sale**
(free), plus **state "blue sky" notice filings** where investors reside (fees vary).

**4.5 Update the cap table**; issue certificates/book-entries; countersign the
Voting Agreement.

> ⚠️ Phase 4 is the one to NOT fully free-hand. Botched Reg D / Form D / blue-sky
> = investor rescission rights + liability. Use Clerky/Carta to generate the round
> + auto-file Form D, or a flat-fee startup lawyer for the closing. Everything
> upstream you can do yourself.

## Order of operations (dependency chain)
0. Confirm 83(b) · gather records (Phase 0)
1. Incorporator handoff + ratification (Phase 1)
2. **File dual-class A&R while sole stockholder** (Phase 2)
3. Advisor grants + voting agreement (Phase 3)
4. Round + Form D (Phase 4)
5. File trademarks anytime (parallel — USPTO TEAS, ~$250–350/class/mark)

blue-sky $0–300/state · platform (Clerky/Carta/Atlas) ~$0–500 · 83(b) mailing free.
Total well under a full legal engagement — with counsel reserved for review of the
two ⚠️ items only.
