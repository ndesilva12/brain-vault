# D3 Model — Revised Thesis + Six-School Experiment Set
_Session: 2026-07-28 · supersedes the pessimism in the D3 feasibility memo after Norman's
pushback. Assesses the six schools Norman wants to experiment with: NYU, UMass Boston,
Williams, Amherst, Johns Hopkins, WashU St. Louis. Ties source-checked (standing rule)._

## Revised thesis — where Norman's pushback moves me
Conceded (I was too tournament-centric and too gloomy):
- **The Dance was never the whole point.** Story + commercial viability in years 1-4 is a
  product on its own — Wrexham S1 was a hit with zero promotion.
- **Timeline is clean.** Apply Feb 2027 (moratorium lifts) → play D3 in **2027-28 (season 1)**
  → become a reclassifying D1 member → ~4-yr transition = a natural **4-5 season arc**. Winning
  the first D1 conference (even tournament-ineligible) is a legitimate season climax.
- **Cost is the feature.** $750K-$2M SPV vs $10-12M at D1. With production self-funded, the
  **downside is floored → any revenue is high-MOIC upside.** Materially better risk profile.
- **No-scholarship isn't fatal.** NIL is permitted at D3; a signing bonus can cover
  attendance + a premium, papered into the contract. **Being cast in a Netflix show is genuine
  NIL value**, which *strengthens* the "valid business purpose" defense against the July-2025
  College Sports Commission scrutiny.

## Where I still push (real risks)
1. **The move is will-gated at the school — and 4 of the 6 are the least-willing schools
   alive.** Williams, Amherst, WashU (and largely NYU) are NESCAC/UAA **D3-purists**: D3 status
   is institutional identity, not a price. Capital doesn't move ideology. If the D1 arc matters,
   these are the wrong horses.
2. **The $750K floor fits a cheap public, not the elite privates.** NYU/Williams/Amherst/
   Hopkins/WashU run **~$85-92K all-in**. Covering attendance for ~13 players ≈ **$1.1M+ before
   any talent premium → realistically $1.5-2M** there. $750K-$1M only works at a public
   (**UMass Boston**, ~$15-30K COA).
3. **Don't fold NIL into the production budget.** That hands EverWonder basketball-ops
   financing = a backend-capture vector (per our risk map). **Keep NIL in the SPV** — at $1-2M
   it's trivially fundable by the celebrity, a title sponsor, or a streaming advance, IP
   firewall intact. Same low-cost conclusion, cleaner structure.
4. **"Sponsorship comparable" is half-true.** Celebrity/streaming-halo brand deals ≈
   tier-independent (comparable early). Inventory/activation money (signage, local corporate,
   ticketing) is market/eyeball-bound and lags until the story scales.
5. **Compliance line:** NIL can't be a pre-enrollment inducement, and payment must be genuine
   NIL (not a disguised athletic scholarship, which D3 bars). The show supplies real NIL value;
   Loeb papers it.

## Net position
More bullish than my first memo. The D3 experiment is a **legitimate, low-downside swing** —
but **pick the school for the outcome you want:**
- **Full D3→D1→eventual-Dance arc?** You need a school that will actually move. Only **Johns
  Hopkins** and **UMass Boston** realistically qualify. The four purists won't.
- **Pure D3 content/commercial play (D1 optional)?** **NYU** is best by far (market + wattage +
  MSG history). WashU/Williams/Amherst are charming but sub-scale.

### Johns Hopkins — the realistic-arc bet (dark horse)
**Already fields D1 lacrosse** (Big Ten) → it has crossed the D1 threshold and owns the
infrastructure/governance experience; a **D1 basketball move is more conceivable here than at
any other elite academic.** **Bloomberg '64 has given $3.35B+ — the most any individual has
given any US university** — the ultimate institutional catalyst if he wanted a program. Caveat:
Bloomberg is a *catalyst, not on-camera talent* — solve the on-camera celebrity separately
(Baltimore: **David Simon** — *The Wire*, thematically perfect; **Jada Pinkett Smith** — Balt.
native). Market mid. **If the D1 arc is the goal, this is the strongest structural bet.**

### NYU — the wattage/market ceiling
Best *show* by far. NYC (max market) + **Billy Crystal** (verified alum, Basketball HOF
superfan) / **Adam Sandler** (alum, made *Hustle*) / **Lady Gaga** (Tisch). Buried hook that
reframes the pitch: **NYU was a 1940s-60s basketball power at Madison Square Garden** before
de-emphasizing → **"return to glory," not just a climb**, with MSG as a ready marquee venue.
Move realism low-moderate (size could support it; barrier is 50 years of zero athletic
ambition). **Even if D1 never happens, it's a great NYC D3 series** — the flagship concept pick.

### UMass Boston — the cost-efficient, willing mover
Public, urban, growth-minded, **no D3 ideology** → the most *willing* institutional profile,
and **cheapest to field** (public COA). Boston market strong. **Two real blockers:** (1) thin
celebrity alumni → needs a regional Boston name, most already slotted (Wahlberg/Burr/Affleck/
Damon at Northeastern; Cena/Schreiber at UMass Amherst); (2) **UMass-system politics** — Amherst
is the system's D1 flagship, so a second D1 hoops program at the Boston campus is an internal
fight. Net: most economically efficient + willing, but solve celebrity + politics.

### WashU St. Louis — content play, unlikely arc
UAA purist → won't move. **Andy Cohen** = regional (STL native + deep WashU family + 2026
commencement speaker — *not* an alum), big personality + real sports fan. Mid market. Fine
content, no arc.

### Williams / Amherst — weakest of the six
D3 athletic blue bloods but **NESCAC = the hardest anti-D1 ideology → move realism ~zero.**
Celebrities are respected character actors (**Bradley Whitford**/**Strathairn** at Williams;
**Teller**/**Brian Doyle-Murray** at Amherst — the latter is Bill Murray's brother, a mild
cross-thread), not mega-wattage. Rural, no market. **Weak on both axes** — charming one-offs,
not pilots.

## Recommendation — run TWO pilots, not six
1. **NYU** — the wattage/market ceiling (MSG "return-to-glory" concept; move aspirational).
2. **Johns Hopkins** — the realistic-arc bet (existing D1 lacrosse + Bloomberg; solve on-camera
   celebrity separately).
- **Backup:** UMass Boston if you want a genuinely likely mover and can solve celebrity + system
  politics.
- **Deprioritize** Williams / Amherst / WashU for a D1-arc goal — least likely to move, and
  without the market/wattage to carry a D3-only play at scale.
- **Sequence** after Davidson / St. Joe's prove the format and land the streamer relationship —
  then a D3 pilot is a cheap, on-brand swing with the downside already floored.
