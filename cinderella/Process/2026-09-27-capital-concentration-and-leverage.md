# Capital concentration — how much leverage does Jesse actually have?

_2026-09-27. Norman's concern: TCG is the furthest-along capital source, and being the only real
door hands Jesse leverage. Assessment and the antidotes._

---

## First — separate two different problems

Norman may be conflating them:

| Problem | Difficulty |
|---|---|
| Replacing **Peter Chernin** — a top-tier media operator with a fund, relationships and sector credibility | **Hard.** There are maybe a dozen in the world |
| Replacing **a ~$3M SPV check** | ⭐ **Not hard at all** |

**Per our own one-pager the strategic capital partner is in for roughly $3M per team**, with about
$12M expected from sponsors. TCG is not the door to $15M. It is the door to $3M.

That single fact collapses most of the leverage worry.

## Where leverage actually comes from

Not from being the only source. **From being the only source *at the moment you need it*.**

Three antidotes, in order of power:

1. ⭐ **Reduce the amount needed.** Already the plan — sponsors carry ~80%, and under
   `2026-09-13-nil-sponsor-architecture-and-pcsa.md` they contract **directly with players**, so
   that money never has to be raised by MC at all.
2. ⭐ **Have two or three warm alternatives** so nobody is the only door. Not signed — just warm
   enough to name.
3. ⭐⭐ **Never be on a clock someone else set.** Urgency is what converts interest into leverage.

## The structure already protects this — do not undo it

## ⭐⭐ The real leverage flip is the streamer, not another investor

Today Jesse is being asked to fund a show that **might not exist**. That is why he wants Curry
proof, and it is why his interest is conditional.

**After a platform commits, the question changes** from "will this be a show" to "do you want in
on a show that is happening." At that point capital appears and no single partner is the door.

**Boardwalk is the shortest path to that commitment** — ~85% odds of real rooms, Netflix-heavy.
Which means: **the fix for capital concentration runs through the producer, not through
prospecting more investors.**

## What to do

1. **Nothing adversarial.** Jesse has asked for nothing unreasonable and has been flexible —
   he already agreed to Unanimous co-producing. This is anticipatory worry, not a live problem.
   The answer to anticipatory worry is **quietly building optionality**, not renegotiating a
   relationship that is working.
2. **Ask Fried about Shamrock on Tuesday.** Lowest-cost new capital path available.
3. **Revive Pravin at RedBird** — answer the production-cost question EverWonder never answered.
4. **Separate what Jesse provides:** money (~$3M, replaceable) · credibility (valuable) ·
   relationships (valuable). **Do not pay for the second and third with terms on the first.**
5. **Never signal that he is the only source** — and do not behave as if he is. Urgency reads as
   weakness and manufactures the leverage.
