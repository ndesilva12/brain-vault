Not from being the only source. **From being the only source *at the moment you need it*.**

Three antidotes, in order of power:

1. ⭐ **Reduce the amount needed.** Already the plan — sponsors carry ~80%, and under

2. ⭐ **Have two or three warm alternatives** so nobody is the only door. Not signed — just warm
   enough to name.
3. ⭐⭐ **Never be on a clock someone else set.** Urgency is what converts interest into leverage.

## ⭐⭐ The real leverage flip is the streamer, not another investor

Today Jesse is being asked to fund a show that **might not exist**. That is why he wants Curry
proof, and it is why his interest is conditional.

**After a platform commits, the question changes** from "will this be a show" to "do you want in
on a show that is happening." At that point capital appears and no single partner is the door.

**Boardwalk is the shortest path to that commitment** — ~85% odds of real rooms, Netflix-heavy.
Which means: **the fix for capital concentration runs through the producer, not through
prospecting more investors.**

   The answer to anticipatory worry is **quietly building optionality**, not renegotiating a
