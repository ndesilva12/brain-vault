# Revenue Vectors & Downside Hedges
_Session: 2026-07-20 · beyond production + team revenue share_

The core thesis: **team revenue share is the narrowest, riskiest slice of the
value MC creates** (it depends on winning). Build a **"relevance royalty," not a
"winning royalty"** — price the *attention* MC manufactures (enrollment, giving,
tourism, brand), which the Netflix series produces even if the team loses in March.
Each party that benefits is a potential payer.

Ranges are per school-season unless noted; all PROJECTED, negotiable.

---

## 1. Enrollment / tuition-lift fee  ·  win-independent
"Flutie Effect" is measured: a deep run lifts applications 2–10% (George Mason '06
~+22%, FGCU '13 ~+30%, Butler ~+40% over its run). A Netflix series is a bigger,
longer front door. Dollars are in **net tuition** (less discounting, better yield,
out-of-state/international mix at 2–3×), not the app count. Capturable incremental
net tuition ≈ **$2–8M/yr** at a mid-major.
- **Flat "enrollment-marketing" partner fee (cleanest):** $250K–1M/yr.
- **% of measured incremental net tuition:** 5–15% over baseline, 3–5-yr term → $100K–1.2M/yr.
- **Per-unit:** $50–150 / incremental app; $2,000–5,000 / incremental out-of-state enrollee.
- **Best:** base fee + milestone bonuses; pre-agree baseline & measurement window.
- Needs **president/provost**, not just the AD.

## 2. D2C merch + experiences  ·  partly win-dependent
D2C *owns the margin* (licensing royalties don't). Wrexham merch ran into the
millions post-doc. Gross merch **$1–5M base / $5–15M reach**; margin 40–55%.
Franchise-branded ("Making Cinderella") + player NIL SKUs compound across teams.
- MC owns D2C store/JV: 40–55% margin, pay school ~8–15% marks royalty (inverse deal). Net **$0.5–3M/yr**.
- Co-branded (school IP): 50/50 gross profit. Print-on-demand kills inventory risk (~20–35% margin, zero downside).
- **Experiences:** VIP/hospitality, meet-the-celebrity, travel, fan fest. 500–2,000 pkgs × $500–2,500 = $0.5–3M gross, 50%+ margin; marquee celeb event $100–500K.
- **Combined net to MC: ~$0.5–4M/yr.**

## 3. Tourism / economic-development partner (+ film credit)  ·  win-independent
Payers already have budgets: city/county, **state tourism board**, **CVB/DMO**
(budgets $2–10M+/yr, hotel-tax funded), econ-dev authorities, tourism sponsors.
- **Film tax credit / rebate — do first, free money:** most states rebate **20–30% of qualified in-state production spend** → $0.5–3M straight off production cost. Pick school locations partly for the incentive.
- **Destination-marketing integration fee** (CVB/state pays to be featured in the series): $250K–2M/yr.
- **Tourism-category presenting sponsor** (hotel/airline/OTA): $500K–3M/yr.
- **Best:** stack film credit + integration fee. Town gets famous win or lose.

## 4. Owned MTE / tournament event  ·  partly win-dependent
Early-season MTEs (Maui, Battle 4 Atlantis, Players Era paying $1M+/team) are
promoter-owned — they keep gate/title/media, pay teams appearance fees. Own it →
own the stack; it doubles as **series content + recruiting/transfer-eval hub**.
- Title sponsor $0.5–3M · media/streaming $0.5–5M · gate $0.5–3M · other $0.25–1M.
- Less venue + appearance fees (7 teams × $100–300K) + production/ops.
- **MC net: $1–5M/yr** (one event); scales as a franchise. **De-risks the live-game line** (own the inventory).
- Structures: own outright (pay appearance fees) or JV a promoter (ESPN Events/Intersport), MC 30–50% as the draw.

## 5. University-wide fundraising lift  ·  win-independent
Distinct from the basketball-donor share — the *whole university's* giving lift.
Must be a **services fee, never "a cut of gifts."**
- Development-marketing partnership fee: $250K–1M/yr (campaign-counsel-style; CCS/GG&A charge $500K–several M).
- Milestone bonus: 1–3% of measured incremental giving, capped, counsel-blessed.
- **Bundle #1 + #5 into one "Institutional Value Partnership"** with the university (enrollment + development + brand): ~$0.5–2M/yr base + bonuses, up to $1–3M/yr all-in. One champion (the president), cleaner optics.

## 6. Realignment success fee  ·  win-independent but uncertain
Conference jump multiplies media (Gonzaga WCC ~$0.5M/school → power seat $7–15M+).
School won't cede perpetual media, so time-box or one-time.
- One-time placement/success fee: $1–5M flat.
- Time-limited share of incremental media: 10–20% for 3–5 yrs (~$2–8M total), then reverts. = the Promotion SPV.
- Advisory retainer + success fee; same playbook advises other schools (consulting line).
- **Option, not base case** — slow, political, partly outside MC's control; assignability/consent hurdles.

## Summary

| # | Vector | Range to MC | Win-dep? | Cleanliness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enrollment/tuition-lift fee | $250K–1M/yr (+ up to ~$1.2M on %) | No | Medium (provost) |
| 2 | D2C merch + experiences | $0.5–4M/yr net | Partly | Easy (own it) |
| 3 | Tourism / econ-dev + film credit | $0.75–5M/yr (credit $0.5–3M) | No | Easy (new payer) |
| 4 | Owned MTE event | $1–5M/yr net | Partly | Medium (build/JV) |
| 5 | University fundraising lift | $250K–1M/yr (+ bonus) | No | Hard (optics) |
| 6 | Realignment success fee | $1–5M one-time / $2–8M over 3–5 yr | No (uncertain) | Hard (political) |

**Takeaways:** (a) bundle **#1 + #5** into one Institutional Value Partnership with
the university president — biggest, most win-proof pool. (b) The **film tax credit
(#3)** is the fastest, cleanest dollar — pick schools partly for state incentives;
it drops straight to the production P&L.

**Modeled (#1–#4) as optional ancillary vectors:** ~**$2.5M base / ~$15M reach** per
school-season to the venture, layered on top of Bucket 1 + Bucket 2. See the
"Ancillary Vectors" tab in 2026-07-15-three-school-revenue-projection.xlsx.
Booking: merch/MTE → SPV (team-adjacent, split by equity); enrollment/tourism →
parent/MC (institutional deals, MC keeps ~all).
