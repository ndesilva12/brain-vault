## The two archetype MMR structures
- **Model A — Guarantee / rights-fee** (Learfield classic, bigger schools): agency
  pays a fixed annual rights fee, keeps sales until fee + costs covered, then
  splits overage (school 40–60%). Mid-term surges mostly stay with the agency —
  the "guarantee trap."
- **Model B — Revenue-share / agency** (Van Wagner's typical mid-major deal —
  almost certainly SJU): little/no guarantee; 1–2 sellers ("GM") on campus; **net**
  sponsorship revenue split after seller costs recovered. Typical: **school
  50–70% / agency 30–50%** of net; sometimes a small floor ($100–300K).

## Jersey patch
New inventory (conferences recently began permitting one commercial patch);
typically sits **inside the MMR agency's inventory unless carved out**. Mid-major
market: **$100–500K/yr** (power schools $1–3M+). Terms short (**1–3 yrs**) —
everyone expects repricing. Mostly unsold/underpriced at the A-10 level.

## Team apparel
Mid-majors get **product, not cash**: ~5-yr Nike/adidas/UA deal (often via a team
dealer like BSN), **$200–500K/yr in product allowance/discounts**, little/no cash.
→ why the model holds this line flat mid-term.

## Merchandise / licensing
Via licensing agent (CLC/Learfield Licensing or Fanatics): **~10–12% royalty on
wholesale**; SJU nets ~**$100–300K/yr**. Fanatics runs the online store; school
gets royalty only — no margin, no customer data. (The D2C opening — see merch note.)

## Why this is GOOD for MC — 3 implications
1. **No guarantee trap.** Under rev-share, incremental sponsorship flows to the
   school's books (their 50–70% of net) → lands in **Bucket 1**, so the 80/20
   touches it. The Learfield fear mostly doesn't apply at SJU.
2. **Originator carve-out is easier.** Van Wagner = commission seller with 1–2
   campus staff. MC arriving with national Netflix-priced sponsors they could
   never source → **MC keeps ~85% as originator, VW takes a processing slice** is
   a natural deal; they earn fees on money that doesn't exist without MC.
3. **Timing leverage.** 2015-vintage deal → renewal window live. **The school LOI
   should require the MMR renewal/amendment to include:** (a) the MC originator
   carve-out; (b) the jersey patch reserved to the SPV or priced at the national
   tier; (c) consent for neutral-site / relocated-game inventory.
