## Queens eligibility — corrected

Queens **did not** make the 2026 NCAA Tournament. What's true: Jan-2025 NCAA legislation
cut the D1 transition from 4 years to 3; Queens got **full D1 status in July 2025** and is
**tournament-eligible from the 2025-26 season onward** — so it clears the tier-doc's
transition screen and would be **fully eligible for our inaugural 2027-28 season.** The
value is the arc (D2→D1→contender in record time = purest underdog story); the catch is
platform, not eligibility.

## Per-school read

**Loyola Chicago — the strongest all-around platform.** A-10 (100% unit retention, same
accretive math as Davidson/St. Joe's/VCU), a **major Chicago media market**, and — uniquely
— **it already owns the Cinderella brand**: the 2018 Sister Jean Final Four is the literal
template for this franchise. Gentile Arena is ~4,500 (small → United Center relocation for
marquee games is easy in-market). The one asterisk: its Cinderella moment *already
happened*, so the arc is "return to glory," not "first-ever" — less novel transformation
delta than a true unknown. But on economics + brand + market + celebrity depth it's the
best of the six. **Deep Chicago celebrity bench** (below).

**VCU — best economics, weakest *story*.** A-10 100% units and real credibility: 2011
Final Four, "Havoc," sells out the 7,600-seat Siegel Center. **But VCU isn't a Cinderella**
— it's an established A-10 contender with a ~$3M+ program. Low transformation delta = less
compelling TV, and the high baseline shrinks the SPV's 80%-of-increment upside. A
fallen-giant/brand play. Celebrity bench thin.

**UNC Charlotte — market over story.** American tier: buried for at-large, AAC auto-bid
winnable. Real edge is the **Charlotte corporate market** (BofA/Wells/Truist) + a
marquee-relocation venue in town (Spectrum Center). Moderate arc — some drought, no
sustained success. Clean alum tie in Clay Aiken.

**Bradley — "fallen giant" lite.** MVC is a strong mid-major, which cuts against us: it's a
**multi-bid league → bubble risk**, not a one-bid auto-bid lock. Peoria = small corporate
market (Caterpillar). Story is former-glory (1950 runner-up, 2006 Sweet 16). Thin star bench.

**Rider — textbook auto-bid Cinderella, no arena and no star.** MAAC one-bid league →
**auto-bid near-lock** (the framework's single biggest strategic edge) and **high story
purity** (minimal tournament history = big transformation delta). NYC/NJ proximity helps
relocation (Prudential Center). **Killer: ~1,700-seat Alumni Gym** — relocation is
mandatory. The Jon Stewart hometown hook (below) is a genuine asset.

**Queens (Charlotte) — purest arc, thinnest platform.** ASUN one-bid → auto-bid path, best
ready-made D2→D1 story of the group, eligible for our 2027-28 season. But it's a brand-new
D1, **~2,500-enrollment private school** — minimal commercial infrastructure, tiny arena,
thinnest sponsor/gate base of the six. Same Charlotte market as UNCC.

## Bottom line

- **Celebrity depth flips the ranking.** Loyola Chicago (Chicago bench) and the two
  Charlotte schools now have real, source-able regional options; Rider gains a bullseye in
  Jon Stewart; VCU/Bradley remain the thinnest. Cleanest **alum** ties: Clay Aiken (UNCC),
  Tom Papa (Rider), David Baldacci/Boris Kodjoe (VCU), Hersey Hawkins (Bradley). Everything
  punchier is regional — which Norman has explicitly OK'd, still source-check each.
- **Framework-fit ranking (story + auto-bid + economics + gettable celebrity):**
  1. **Loyola Chicago** — best all-around: A-10 economics, iconic Cinderella brand, major
     market, deep celebrity bench. Caveat: it's "return to glory," not a first-ever arc.
  2. **Queens** — purest live transformation arc, auto-bid path, eligible 2027-28; weakest
     platform to build on.
  3. **Rider** — classic one-bid auto-bid Cinderella; solve the arena (relocate) — Stewart
     is a real celebrity anchor.
  4. **VCU** — best raw economics/credibility, but low transformation delta + thin celebrity;
     a brand/credibility anchor, not the underdog.
  5. **UNC Charlotte** — market + AAC upside; modest story; strong regional celebrity trio.
  6. **Bradley** — MVC multi-bid dilutes the auto-bid edge; thin star bench.
- **Portfolio read (per the "mix" thesis):** pair a brand/credibility anchor (**Loyola** or
  VCU — A-10, 100% units) with a pure underdog (**Queens** or **Rider**). Loyola is the
  standout add — it's the only one of the six that already *is* the Cinderella story.
