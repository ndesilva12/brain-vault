# School Tier Comparison — Low-Major vs Mid-Major (A-10) vs American
_Session: 2026-07-21 · pros/cons for target selection by tier (low-major = NEC /
Big South / America East level; mid-major = A-10 level; high-major non-power =
American level)_

**Eligibility screen (applies to any candidate):** a school in D1 reclassification
(moving up from D2) is **ineligible for the NCAA Tournament for its 4-year
transition**. No March Madness = no Cinderella payoff → transitioning schools are
out for the current cohort, later cohorts at most.

---

## Where the low-major is MORE constrained
1. **Gate ceiling (hard physical cap).** Arenas run ~2,500–4,000 seats. Sold out
   + re-priced ≈ $1.5–2.5M gate vs $3.5–5M at the A-10 tier. Marquee-game
   relocation becomes **mandatory**, not optional — proximity to an NBA-scale
   arena becomes a site-selection criterion.
2. **Organic sponsorship market.** Low-major towns are small corporate markets:
   the school's own sponsor lines run $200–500K gross, not $1.5–3M. With-MC
   Bucket 1 ceiling ~$11–14M instead of $17M+.
3. **Structural money ≈ zero.** Conference distribution ~$100–200K, ESPN+-tier
   media. (Neutral for MC — school keeps structural — but the school is poorer
   overall and leans harder on us.)
4. **Institutional shock absorption.** A $10M roster is a ~50x jump on a
   low-major's NIL reality; 2-person compliance, no production/ticket-ops
   infrastructure — MC stands up a commercial department, not augments one.
5. **Talent-destination friction.** A big city beats a small college town as a
   sell — real but overrated in the NIL era ($500K packages move players;
   the Netflix platform is itself a pitch).

## Where the low-major has MORE upside
1. **Tiny baseline = SPV's favorite fact.** School keeps baseline; we take 80%
   above. Low-major baseline ~$1.2–1.8M vs ~$3.2M at the A-10 tier. Rough math:
   (12 − 1.5) × 0.8 ≈ **$8.4M** vs (17.6 − 3.2) × 0.8 ≈ $11.5M — absolute ceiling
   lower, but not proportionally (the baseline give-back shrinks too).
2. **One-bid-league auto-bid — biggest strategic advantage.** Win the conference
   tourney → guaranteed March Madness. A transformed roster in a one-bid league ≈
   near-lock; ordinary low-major rosters make the Dance this way regularly. At
   the A-10 tier, 24 wins can still miss; at the American tier you're buried by
   the league's brand programs. **Trades seed quality for near-certainty of
   appearing** — and the series needs the appearance; a 13-seed with one upset is
   the perfect arc.
3. **Clean commercial field.** Low-majors usually have **no Van Wagner/Learfield
   deal at all** (sponsorships sold in-house by one staffer). No agency carve-out
   fight, no MMR split, no patch promised. Greenfield → MC keeps ~100% of what it
   originates, not 85%.
4. **Deal leverage & speed.** The president takes the meeting this week; longer
   exclusivity, broader rights (merch/patch/naming), possibly better than 80/20 —
   the counterfactual is a $1.5M program in obscurity. Fastest LOI. And the
   transformation delta is the best TV (purest Cinderella).
5. **Realignment upside more plausible.** One-bid league → CAA/A-10 is a realistic
   jump for a suddenly-national program (Promotion SPV thesis); A-10 → power
   conference is much taller.

## Summary table

| | Low-major tier | A-10 tier | American tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline school keeps | ~$1.2–1.8M | ~$3.2M | ~$5M+ |
| With-MC Bucket 1 ceiling | ~$11–14M | ~$17–18M | ~$18–22M |
| SPV 80% of increment | **~$8–10M** | ~$11–12M | ~$11–13M |
| March Madness path | **Auto-bid near-lock** | Bubble risk | Buried by big dogs |
| MMR encumbrance | None (greenfield) | Agency rev-share (favorable) | Learfield-style (worst) |
| Deal speed/terms | Fastest, best | Moderate | Slowest, worst |
| Story purity | **Highest** | High | Lower |

## Net read
Economics ~15–25% smaller at the low-major tier, but **probability-weighted** the
low-major arguably wins: near-certain tournament appearance, cleaner rights,
faster close, better terms, purer narrative. **Optimal portfolio = mix**: an A-10
anchor (bigger economics, credibility) + a true low-major (certainty + story).
Screen out any school still in D1 transition.
