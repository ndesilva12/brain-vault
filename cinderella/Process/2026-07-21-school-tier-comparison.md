# School Tier Comparison — Low-Major vs Mid-Major (A-10) vs American
_Session: 2026-07-21 · pros/cons for target selection (Longwood/Bryant/Mercyhurst
vs St. Joe's-tier vs Memphis-tier)_

**Verified flag:** Mercyhurst is in D1 reclassification (from D2, Fall 2024) —
**ineligible for the NCAA Tournament until 2028-29.** No March Madness = no
Cinderella payoff → park for a later cohort at most.

Longwood (Big South champs '22, '24) and Bryant (NCAA '22) are full members — clean.

## Where the low-major is MORE constrained
1. **Gate ceiling (hard physical cap).** Bryant Chace ~2,600; Longwood JPB Center
   ~3,000 (new, 2022). Sold out + re-priced ≈ $1.5–2.5M gate vs $3.5–5M at A-10.
   Marquee-game relocation becomes **mandatory** (Providence works for Bryant;
   Longwood is ~60 mi from Richmond).
2. **Organic sponsorship market.** Farmville VA / Smithfield RI / Erie PA are tiny
   corporate markets: school's own sponsor lines $200–500K gross, not $1.5–3M.
   With-MC Bucket 1 ceiling ~$11–14M instead of $17M+.
3. **Structural money ≈ zero.** Conference distro ~$100–200K, ESPN+ media.
   (Neutral for MC — school keeps structural — but school leans harder on us.)
4. **Institutional shock absorption.** $10M roster at Longwood ≈ 50x their NIL
   reality; 2-person compliance, no production/ticket-ops infrastructure — MC
   stands up a commercial department, not augments one.
5. **Talent-destination friction.** Philly beats Erie as a sell — real but
   overrated in the NIL era ($500K packages move players; Netflix is a pitch).

## Where the low-major has MORE upside
1. **Tiny baseline = SPV's favorite fact.** School keeps baseline; we take 80%
   above. Longwood baseline ~$1.2–1.8M vs St. Joe's $3.2M. Rough math:
   (12 − 1.5) × 0.8 ≈ **$8.4M** vs (17.6 − 3.2) × 0.8 ≈ $11.5M — absolute ceiling
   lower, but not proportionally (the baseline give-back shrinks too).
2. **One-bid-league auto-bid — biggest strategic advantage.** Win the conference
   tourney → guaranteed March Madness. Transformed roster in Big South/NEC ≈
   near-lock (Longwood danced twice with ordinary rosters). A-10 = bubble risk
   at 24 wins; American = buried by Memphis. **Trades seed quality for
   near-certainty of appearing** — and the series needs the appearance; a
   13-seed with one upset is the perfect arc.
3. **Clean commercial field.** Usually **no Van Wagner/Learfield deal at all**
   (in-house, one staffer). No agency carve-out fight, no MMR split, no patch
   promised. Greenfield → MC keeps ~100% of what it originates, not 85%.
4. **Deal leverage & speed.** President takes the meeting this week; longer
   exclusivity, broader rights (merch/patch/naming), possibly better than 80/20 —
   counterfactual is a $1.5M program in obscurity. Fastest LOI. And the
   transformation delta is the best TV (purest Cinderella).
5. **Realignment upside more plausible.** Big South/NEC → CAA/A-10 is a realistic
   jump for a suddenly-national program (Promotion SPV thesis); A-10 → power
   conference is much taller.

## Net read
Economics ~15–25% smaller at a Longwood/Bryant, but **probability-weighted** the
low-major arguably wins: near-certain tournament appearance, cleaner rights,
faster close, better terms, purer narrative. **Optimal portfolio = mix**: an A-10
anchor (bigger economics, credibility) + a true low-major (certainty + story).
Mercyhurst out until ~2028-29.
