# Donor Track & School-Side P&L — Single Season
_Session: 2026-07-15 · companion to 2026-07-15-base-case-calculation.md_

Single season. No ramp, no decay. Completes the base-case build:
Bucket 1 (done) + Bucket 2 (done) + **donor** + the **school-side P&L** that
signs the LOI.

## Donor track

Revised policy: baseline + general/athletics giving stay 100% with the school.
We only touch the **MC-driven uplift**, via two mechanisms.

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Baseline MBB-designated giving (school keeps 100%) | ~$1.5M |
| Transformed giving (Netflix + celebrity + deep-run halo → comp level) | ~$5.0M |
| **Uplift MC creates** | **~$3.5M** |
| → **Converted** to SPV investment (capital — funds the raise, not P&L) | ~$1.5–2.0M |
| → Remaining philanthropic uplift, **split 50/50** | ~$1.5M → **SPV $0.75M** / school $0.75M |
| One-time transformational gift (SDSU precedent: $5M) | **upside, unmodeled** |

- **SPV take:** ~$0.75–1M P&L **+** ~$1.5–2M would-be gifts converted to SPV equity
  (shrinks the outside raise — the conversion thesis in action).
- **School keeps:** ~$2.25M (baseline + its 50%).
- **Guardrails** (compliance note): counsel blesses; net-new, not cannibalizing the
  annual fund; conversion investors accredited + walled off from recruiting.

Comp anchors: Dayton donor $5.5M · Gonzaga $5.8M · SDSU $4.0M + a one-time $5M gift.

## School-side P&L — the LOI slide

AD's question: "why give up 80% of my commercial upside?" Answer: you never had
that upside, and the SPV pays the $10M that creates it.

| Line (single season) | Status quo | Under the deal |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bucket 1 commercial | $2.0M | **$4.0M** (baseline $2M + 20% of ~$10M increment) |
| Structural — conf media + NCAA units (**100% kept**) | $1.5M | **$2.5M** (deep run spikes units) |
| Donor — MBB-designated | $1.5M | **$2.25M** (baseline + 50% of uplift) |
| Bucket 2 (series/sponsor/live games) | $0 | $0 — *100% SPV; school never had it* |
| **Total revenue** | **$5.0M** | **~$8.75M** |
| NIL/roster cost to compete nationally | ~$10M → **unaffordable, never happens** | **$0 — SPV funds it** |
| **Net position** | Mid-major, ~$5M, no relevance | **+$3.75M rev, $10M cost absorbed, Netflix-featured, nationally relevant; reverts to school after 5 yrs** |

**The clincher:** the school forgoes 80% of the commercial *increment* (~$8M to the
SPV) — but that increment **only exists because the SPV manufactured it** (celebrity
+ Netflix + a $10M roster the school could never fund). The school isn't giving up
what it had; it keeps a bigger baseline-plus, keeps 100% of its swollen
structural/units + donor, and pays **$0** for the transformation. No version of
status quo lets a low-major buy a top-10 roster. That's the pitch.
