## What Skydance Sports is — reference

**A studio**, which is a producer that also finances, and since 2025 one that sits inside a
distributor.

| | Role |
|---|---|
| Netflix | Buyer / platform |
| EverWonder | Production company — makes, then sells to a buyer |
| CAA | Agency — represents talent, packages |
| TCG | Investor / operator |
| **Skydance** | **Studio — makes, finances, and owns a platform** |

- **Skydance Media**, David Ellison, founded 2010. Co-financed and co-produced Paramount's
  tentpoles for years (*Top Gun: Maverick*, *Mission: Impossible*). Always brought money as well
  as production.
- **August 2025: Skydance acquired Paramount.** Ellison runs Paramount Skydance — Paramount
  Pictures, **CBS**, **CBS Sports**, Paramount+, and the cable networks.
- **Skydance Sports**, launched ~2022, run by **Jon Weinbach** — ex-Mandalay Sports Media,
  producer on ***The Last Dance***. Same lineage as **Michael Tollin**, already the vault's
  highest-value unworked intro.

**Three differences from EverWonder:** a balance sheet (can finance, not just shop); a parent that
owns the distributor (Groggel's Texas Tech series goes to Paramount+); and Tier 1 standing
(EverWonder ranks ~#25 on our own list).

⚠️ **Correction to the 08-28 note.** It said *"a Skydance meeting is a producer, a vendor, not a
buyer."* Directionally right but it **understates them**. Groggel has demonstrably gotten a series
greenlit at the platform his parent owns. He is not a buyer, but he is a producer with a very
short walk to one.
