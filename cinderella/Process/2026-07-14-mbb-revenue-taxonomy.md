# D1 Men's Basketball — Revenue Line-Item Taxonomy
_Session: 2026-07-14 · master list of every possible revenue source for a
D1 men's basketball program. Names only — values attached in later passes._

Part of the NCAA team-revenue deep dive. Maps to the official EADA / NCAA
financial-reporting categories **plus** the real-world commercial breakdown
underneath them.

**Reference archetypes for this series** (highest-revenue programs outside the
football power conferences *and* excluding the Big East): **Gonzaga (WCC),
Memphis (American), Dayton (A-10), San Diego State (Mountain West).** These
set the realistic *ceiling* (~$20–25M MBB revenue) a transformed program
climbs toward.

---

## Ticketing & game-day
- Season ticket sales
- Single-game & group ticket sales
- Premium seating (suites, club seats, loge, courtside)
- Postseason ticket revenue (conference tournament, NCAA/NIT)
- Concessions (program's share)
- Parking
- Game-day novelty / program / merchandise sales

## Media & broadcasting
- Conference media-rights distribution (TV/streaming)
- Third-tier / local media rights (school-retained)
- School-controlled streaming
- Radio broadcast rights

## Conference & NCAA distributions
- Conference revenue distribution (media + non-media, usually equal-share)
- Conference tournament revenue share
- NCAA Tournament units / Basketball Performance Fund (paid via the conference)
- NCAA broad-based distributions (grants-in-aid fund, sport-sponsorship fund,
  academic enhancement, student-athlete opportunity fund, equal-conference fund)

## Corporate sponsorship & multimedia rights (MMR)
- Multimedia rights guarantee (Learfield / Playfly / in-house)
- Corporate / category sponsorships (official partners)
- Arena or court naming rights (allocated to MBB)
- Signage / LED / in-arena advertising
- Jersey patch / uniform sponsorship
- Coach's show / radio show sponsorship
- Digital & social sponsorship / content deals

## Philanthropy & donor
- Annual fund / booster (athletic-giving) donations
- Seat-related required donations (priority-seating / PSLs)
- Major gifts & endowment gifts (basketball-specific)
- Capital-campaign gifts (facilities)
- Collective / NIL donor contributions (third-party, program-related)

## Apparel & licensing
- Shoe/apparel contract (Nike / adidas / UA) — cash + product allocation
- Trademark / logo licensing royalties
- Team retail merchandise sales
- Video-game / EA College Basketball royalties

## Guarantees & scheduling
- Guarantee ("buy") game income (paid to play road games)
- Neutral-site / multi-team event (MTE) appearance fees
- Early-season / holiday tournament payouts
- Exhibition / charity game revenue

## Camps, clinics & operations
- Basketball camps & clinics revenue
- Coach's camps
- Facility rentals (arena events, when allocated to MBB)

## Institutional & other support
- Direct institutional / university support
- Student-fee allocation
- Direct state / government support (public schools)
- Athletics endowment / investment income (allocated)
- Third-party in-kind support & third-party-paid compensation
- Other / miscellaneous operating revenue

## NIL / revenue-share era (newer)
- NIL collective revenue (third-party)
- Athlete marketing / activation revenue
- House-settlement revenue-share pool (institution-to-athlete mechanism — context)

## Revenue classification (tagging · 2026-07-14)

- **[C] Commercial / earned** — market-driven, scales with demand & winning.
  *The universe Making Cinderella moves and shares in = Program Commercial
  Revenue (PCR).* Ticketing, sponsorship/MMR, school-retained media/streaming,
  licensing/merch, camps, guarantee games.
- **[S] Structural / distribution** — conference/NCAA formula money; largely
  fixed; not the school's to assign. Conference media distribution, conference
  tournament share, NCAA units (performance-linked but conference-mediated),
  NCAA broad-based funds. *School keeps; realignment is the only real lever here.*
- **[X] Subsidy** — institutional/state support, student fees, endowment income,
  in-kind. Not earned. **Inflates reported totals — must be stripped out** or the
  commercial base is badly overstated.
- **[C\*] Commercial-adjacent (donor)** — annual fund, booster, **seat-related
  priority-seating donations**, major gifts. Moves with success but is
  philanthropic; claiming it is legally/optically nuanced.
- **[T] Third-party / cost-side** — NIL/collective money + House rev-share pool.
  This is roster *funding*, not program revenue.

**Decision (2026-07-14): donor [C\*] is OUT of the rev-share base.** PCR =
ticketing + sponsorship + school-retained media + licensing/merch + camps +
guarantee games only. Cleaner NCAA optics; keeps the "we multiply your
*commercial* revenue" story honest. Donor uplift is noted as a school benefit,
not an SPV claim. *(Analyses will still show the with-donor figure for context.)*
