## The Queens correction (important)

The tier-comparison doc screens out D1-transition schools (4-year tournament
ineligibility). **That screen no longer applies to Queens.** Jan-2025 NCAA
legislation cut the transition to **3 years**; Queens got **full D1 status July
2025**, then **won the ASUN and made the 2026 NCAA Tournament** in its first
eligible season. So the naive "recently transitioned → out" read is **reversed** —
Queens is fully eligible and already has a Dance on the board. It's arguably the
**purest Cinderella narrative of the five** (D2→D1→Dance in record time, ready-made
arc). The catch is infrastructure, not eligibility (below).

## Per-school read

**VCU — best economics, weakest *story*.** A-10 means 100% unit retention (same
accretive math as Davidson/St. Joe's) and real credibility: 2011 Final Four, "Havoc"
brand, sells out the 7,600-seat Siegel Center. **But VCU is not a Cinderella** — it's
already a perennial A-10 contender with a ~$3M+ program. Low transformation delta =
less compelling TV, and the high baseline shrinks the SPV's 80%-of-increment upside.
This is a **"fallen giant"/established-brand play, not the underdog arc.** Celebrity
bench is thin (David Baldacci — author, alum; Boris Kodjoe — actor, alum; Lamb of God
— Richmond band). No A-list authentic tie.

**UNC Charlotte — market over story.** American tier: buried for at-large bids, but
the AAC auto-bid is winnable. Real edge is the **Charlotte corporate market** (Bank of
America / Wells Fargo / Truist) plus a marquee-relocation venue in town (Spectrum
Center, Hornets). Moderate transformation arc — some drought, no sustained success.
Celebrity: **Clay Aiken (VERIFIED alum**, American Idol/Broadway) is the cleanest tie —
real but modest Netflix wattage; Matt Hardy (wrestler, alum). Note Steph Curry is a
Charlotte native but he's committed to Davidson.

**Bradley — "fallen giant" lite.** MVC is a strong mid-major, but that cuts against us:
it's a **multi-bid league → bubble risk**, not a one-bid auto-bid lock. Peoria is a
small corporate market (Caterpillar). Story is former-glory (1950 runner-up, 2006 Sweet
16). Celebrity is the weak point: **Shaun Livingston (REGIONAL** — Peoria native, NBA
champ, now Warriors exec; *not* a Bradley alum — went prep-to-pros) is the best angle
and overlaps Norman's NBA world; Hersey Hawkins (**VERIFIED** Bradley legend, less
wattage). No mainstream A-lister.

**Rider — textbook auto-bid Cinderella, no arena and no star.** MAAC is a true one-bid
league → **auto-bid near-lock**, the framework's single biggest strategic advantage,
and **high story purity** (minimal tournament history = huge transformation delta).
NYC/NJ proximity helps marquee relocation (Prudential Center). **Killer: ~1,700-seat
Alumni Gym** — brutal gate ceiling, relocation is mandatory not optional. Celebrity is
thin: Tom Papa (**VERIFIED** alum, comedian), Digger Phelps (**VERIFIED** alum,
ESPN/coach). NJ regional stars exist but **no authentic Rider tie** — don't fabricate one.

**Queens (Charlotte) — purest arc, thinnest platform.** ASUN one-bid → auto-bid path,
**already proven ('26 Dance)**, and the best ready-made Cinderella story of the group.
But it's a **brand-new D1, ~2,500-enrollment private school** — minimal commercial
infrastructure, tiny arena, thinnest sponsor/gate base of the five (this is exactly the
"MC stands up a department, not augments one" low-major profile, at the extreme). Same
Charlotte market as UNCC. Celebrity bench is thin (small alumni base). High story, low
platform.

## Bottom line

- **The honest headline: celebrity is the universal weak spot.** None of the five has an
  A-list, source-verified authentic tie comparable to Curry/Davidson or the St. Joe's
  Philly bench. Best clean alum ties are **Clay Aiken (UNCC)** and **Tom Papa (Rider)** —
  both modest wattage. Everything stronger is regional (no institutional tie) and would
  have to be manufactured, which the standing rule forbids.
- **Framework-fit ranking (story + auto-bid + clean commercial):**
  1. **Queens** — purest arc, proven auto-bid, but weakest platform. Best *story*, hardest *build*.
  2. **Rider** — classic one-bid Cinderella; solve the arena (relocate) and find a real NJ tie.
  3. **VCU** — best economics/credibility, but it's a *fallen-giant* play, not an underdog; low TV delta.
  4. **UNC Charlotte** — market + AAC upside; modest story and celebrity.
  5. **Bradley** — fallen-giant-lite; multi-bid league dilutes the auto-bid edge; thin star bench.
- **If forced to pick one to pursue:** **Rider or Queens** for a true Cinderella arc;
  **VCU** only if the portfolio wants an established A-10 brand for credibility (pair it
  with a real underdog, per the "mix" thesis). All five need a celebrity solved before
  any external step.
