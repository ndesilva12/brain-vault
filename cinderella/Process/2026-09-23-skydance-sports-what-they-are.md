# Skydance Sports — what they are (2026-09-23)

Public-clean Q&A. No phones, no email bodies, no meeting date, no deal terms.

**Live status.** Kitty Gambel replied to Jeremy’s ~Sep 17 submission. The same night, ~8pm ET, she asked for a meeting with Greg Groggel on creative and production. Nothing is scheduled. Nothing is offered.

**Read this as.** Skydance Sports is a Tier-1 sports content studio and producer-packager inside Paramount Sports Entertainment. It is not a streamer and it is not the buyer. It can make the show and sell it to Paramount+ or to a third party.

Norman’s clarifying questions, logged 2026-09-23, are answered below from public sources plus standing Cinderella Corp doctrine.

---

## 1. What Skydance Sports is

A premium sports studio. Launched 2021 under Jon Weinbach. Since the Paramount–Skydance combination it sits inside **Paramount Sports Entertainment (PSE)**, the division Jesse Sisgold runs. PSE’s own site calls Skydance Sports “the industry-leading narrative sports content studio” housed in a division that “creates programming both for Paramount’s portfolio and for external buyers, including global networks, streamers, and distributors.”

PSE complements CBS Sports. CBS Sports is the live-rights arm. Skydance Sports is the film, series, and documentary arm. A meeting with Kitty or Greg is a producer meeting. The license check, if one comes, is written by a platform.

---

## 2. The Amazon analogy — partially right

The rhyme people reach for: Kitty just left Amazon MGM Alternative, and Skydance now sits inside the company that owns Paramount+, so a Skydance show “just goes to Paramount+” the way an Amazon Studios show goes to Prime.

Half of that is fair.

- She did come from inside Amazon’s unscripted studio (SVP, Unscripted and Alternative TV at MGM Alternative; Deadline, Aug 26, 2026). Studio-inside-a-platform-owner is the right shape.
- After the merger, PSE is built to feed Paramount’s own platforms first. Sisgold has said they are the priority supplier for the home channels, and that a significant amount of the slate is still for third parties (The Wrap).

The half that fails:

- **No automatic Prime-style lock-in, and there wasn’t one before the merger either.** The NFL joint venture (announced Nov 2022) picked Skydance because it was *not* exclusive to a platform. Jon Weinbach, SBJ, Sept 18, 2023: “What the NFL really was looking for was the independence. In other words, we were not tied exclusively to any of the platforms, so we could sell to everyone… They could have done this directly with Amazon or directly with Apple, but then in a certain way they would kind of close off their selling opportunity.”
- **Post-merger, home-field advantage is real and third-party sales are still the stated job.** THR’s Sept 11, 2025 launch story: Paramount said PSE will produce scripted and unscripted programming “for its own platforms as well as third-party media companies and streaming services,” and that the division houses Skydance Sports. The PSE site still says external buyers in 2026. Proof after the merger, not just the press release: Apple TV premiered *The Dynasty: UConn Huskies* on Aug 21, 2026, and Apple’s release calls it a Skydance Sports production “housed under Paramount Sports Entertainment.”

Use them as a packager who can aim at Paramount+ or shop out. Do not treat a Skydance yes as a Paramount+ order.

## 4. What a Paramount+ home would mean for *Making Cinderella*

No offer exists. This is the category, not a term sheet.

- **License fee.** A Paramount+ home is a distribution license from a platform that pays for programming. Skydance does not become the buyer because it introduced the show. Who writes the check, for what term, and into which entity (parent vs. season SPV) is a negotiation that has not started.
- **Studio stamp.** Skydance Sports on the card is a real mark: *Air*, *America’s Team*, *Good Rivals*, the UConn dynasty series. Useful with talent, schools, and the next buyer. The stamp is not ownership.
- **Reach.** Smaller than Netflix (~325M) and smaller than Prime’s undisclosed-but-much-larger video reach. About 82 million global paid subscribers is not “nobody.” CBS’s college-basketball window is the strategic reason a Paramount home can still be the right room for a college show even at that sub count. It is not a reason to skip a larger buyer if one is live.
- **Keep both doors open.** A packager who can sell to P+ *and* to Netflix, Amazon, or Apple is more valuable than a captive in-house unit. Competitive tension stays the point until a platform actually bids.

## 5. Can Skydance sell to Netflix, Hulu, Amazon?

Yes. That is the track record, including after they sat inside Paramount.

- **Netflix** — *America’s Team: The Gambler and His Cowboys* (streaming on Netflix; Skydance Sports / NFL).
- **Amazon / Prime Video** — *Air* (Amazon Studios / Skydance; theatrical, then Prime), *Good Rivals* (Prime Video), *Kelce* (Prime Video).
- **Hulu** — *The Land*, Dan Fogelman’s NFL family drama, ordered at Hulu; 20th Television and Skydance Sports produce (announced Oct 2024; title confirmed Jan 2026). Scripted, not a doc, and not yet a released series. It still shows a Hulu sale with the Skydance Sports label on it.
- **Apple TV** — *The Dynasty: UConn Huskies*, premiered Aug 21, 2026. Cleanest post-merger example: PSE-housed studio, Apple buyer, college basketball.

Also on the slate, for the “sell to everybody” point: *NFL Draft: The Pick Is In* for The Roku Channel.

**IP posture does not move because the producer has good buyers.** Standing doctrine, unchanged by this thread:

- **Cinderella Corp is the principal.** It owns the format, the brand, and the franchise.
- A producer is a **service vendor**. Fee for production. **No franchise equity. No format ownership. No parent-company backend.**
- A platform can be offered a **bounded distribution license** (term, territory, window, reversion). That conversation is with the buyer. It is not a grant of the franchise to Skydance, Kitty, Greg, or PSE.
- Same line already drawn on EverWonder: casual “we’ll package it” language does not become format equity.

## 6. Kitty and Greg — who is in the meeting

**Kitty Gambel — SVP, Alternative Unscripted, Skydance Sports.** Start date Sept 8, 2026. Reports to Jon Weinbach, Head of Unscripted. Previous seat: SVP, Unscripted and Alternative TV at Amazon’s MGM Alternative, creative lead across *The Voice*, *Survivor*, *Shark Tank*, *Beat Shazam*, and *American Gladiators* (Deadline, Aug 26, 2026). Her chair is development and sales for lifestyle, competition, and sports-adjacent unscripted. She is the door. She is not Paramount+.

**Greg Groggel — SVP, Unscripted, Skydance Sports.** Public profile lists the SVP title in 2026; he joined the studio in Oct 2023 as VP, Unscripted, from the Olympic Channel. Producer on Netflix’s *The Redeem Team*. Kitty asked for him specifically on creative and production. That is the meeting. It is not on the calendar.

Weinbach remains the head of the unscripted studio above both of them. Sisgold runs PSE above the studio.
