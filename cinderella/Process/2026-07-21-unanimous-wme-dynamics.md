# Unanimous / WME Dynamics — Incentive Read & Claw-Back Playbook
_Session: 2026-07-21 · sensitive — internal strategy note_

## What happened
- **David Sherman (WME, unscripted)** advised: make it **on spec**, "nobody gets
  paid until we sell it" — while believing MC had ~$10M in hand. I.e., MC funds
  and carries all risk; the sale runs through them later.
- **Erick Peyton (Unanimous)** and Sherman have already **spoken to Jesse Jacobs
  (The Chernin Group) without Norman.**
- Posture: they treat MC as **someone to include** in their monetization of
  Norman's idea — "you'll be at the table for anything that happens" — rather
  than partners in Cinderella Corp's franchise. Patronizing, and structurally
  suspicious.

## The incentive read (why they behave this way)
- **Steph content is pre-sold in their minds** (Underrated → Apple). Finished
  Steph product clears the market; spec = minimal risk to them, max price and
  control at the sale moment. The risk isn't gone — it's moved onto MC.
- **"Nobody gets paid" = MC is the financier.** Capital takes risk; the
  attachment appreciates; whoever controls the auction controls the waterfall.
  Unanimous's model is *owning* IP around Steph, not joining others' IP.
- **Delaying streamer talks keeps them the gatekeeper** — MC never builds its own
  platform relationships; the payday routes through their hands.
- The spec advice is right for a $3–5M documentary and **wrong for MC's
  enterprise**: the $12M roster must be funded pre-season, and sponsors are
  paying for committed national distribution. Different objective functions.
- They see "a Steph show at Davidson, MC included." We are building "an operating
  company that owns a format; Steph gets the first slot."

## MC's actual leverage
1. **The signed Davidson AD LOI — the queen on the board.** Davidson is exclusive
   to Cinderella Corp for any similar project. Without the school there is no
   Cinderella story — a Steph doc without Davidson is just Underrated 2. They
   control the talent; **we control the venue and the format.**
2. **The format/franchise IP** — the repeatable system (SPV, rev-share, NIL
   engine, 9-school roadmap) that a talent shop cannot replicate or pitch.
3. **Alternative pairings** — other celebrity/school lanes in motion make MC a
   platform, not a Steph-adjacent hopeful.

## Claw-back playbook
1. **Paper the IP now.** Trademark filings (franchise name), copyright the format
   bible/treatment/deck, dated provenance records. **NDA + non-circumvention
   signed by Unanimous/WME before the next material conversation** (Mubadala
   template exists). If one was already signed, review whether the Jacobs/Chernin
   contact breached non-circ. Loeb engaged before further talks.
2. **Meet Chernin directly.** Don't protest the end-around — open MC's own line:
   Cinderella Corp presents the *franchise* deck to TCG (and peers). Only MC can
   sell the 9-school format story; a talent co can only sell one show.
3. **Send THEM paper.** The driver is whoever circulates the term sheet. Offer
   Unanimous a defined lane: co-production credit + fee + Steph's SPV equity
   (20–30%) on the **Davidson series only** — format, franchise, and enterprise
   stay with MC. Real value in their lane, zero IP leakage.
4. **Put a clock on Davidson.** "Cohort 1 has three slots; Davidson's window
   closes [date]. The LOI is exclusive to us — if the attachment isn't papered,
   the slot goes to the next pairing." Scarcity + deadline = driver behavior.
   "We'll come back to you" is a soft-shelve; answer it with a calendar.
5. **Make the second lane real, fast.** Advance Hart/St. Joe's (or Sudeikis/UMKC)
   visibly. The moment another pairing has momentum, the narrative flips from
   "their Steph project" to "MC's franchise — Steph got slot #1."
6. **Never negotiate the show; negotiate the franchise.** Re-anchor every
   conversation to the defined structure: SPV equity slots, production partner
   (EverWonder/W+P drive distribution — not WME), streamer slot, format reserved.

## Red lines

- No further substantive disclosures without NDA/non-circ on file.
- Distribution relationships belong to the venture (via production partner), not
  the talent agency.
