# Base-Case Calculation — Single-Season Per-School Economics
_Session: 2026-07-15 · synthesis of the four archetype builds
(2026-07-14-mbb-revenue-archetype-builds.md) into the model's inputs._

**Single season. No ramp, no decay.** One transformed school, one year.

---

## The mechanic (locked 2026-07-15)

Two SPV revenue streams + a donor track. Keep them separate.

1. **Bucket 1 — the school's own commercial revenue** (gate, concessions, its own
   sponsors/MMR, apparel, licensing, school media, camps, guarantees).
   - School keeps its **baseline** (~$2M for a low-major).
   - **80% of everything above baseline → SPV.** This is the **investor return.**
   - Target: **80% of Bucket 1 ≈ $8–10M.**

2. **Bucket 2 — SPV-procured streams** (streaming series, live games, national
   presenting sponsor + patch, international + format). **100% SPV.** These
   **roughly recoup the $15M SPV cost** — and the series is a real profit center.

3. **Structural (conf media, NCAA units, broad-based):** school keeps 100%.
   Units spike with a deep tournament run — stays home, helps sell the deal.

4. **Donor (C\*):** separate track — conversion (donors → SPV investors) +
   incremental 50/50 on the giving uplift. Not yet sized.

The old **$24M** was simply 80% of an inflated **$30M** Bucket 1. Corrected to
**$8–10M** — comps mature at ~$9–12M total MBB commercial (Gonzaga/Dayton/SDSU/Memphis).

## How a small-arena school reaches ~$12M Bucket 1 (80% ≈ $8–10M)

The bridge: **you can't get there on the home gate alone** (4–5k seats caps you at
~$3–4M). The lever is **relocating 3 marquee games to NBA-scale venues** + national
licensing that only appears with Netflix-level relevance.

| Bucket 1 line | St. Joe's | Davidson | Hawaii |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Home gate (premium-priced, filled) | 2.7 | 3.4 | 6.2 |
| 3 marquee games at big venue | 3.7¹ | 3.5² | 1.0³ |
| Concessions/parking/novelty | 0.8 | 0.8 | 0.8 |
| Sponsorship/MMR (school's own) | 1.8 | 1.8 | 1.5 |
| Licensing/merch (national) | 1.3 | 1.5 | 1.0 |
| School media/streaming | 0.3 | 0.3 | 0.3 |
| Apparel | 0.6 | 0.6 | 0.5 |
| Guarantee/neutral/MTE/tourney | 0.7 | 0.7 | 0.7 |
| Camps/rentals | 0.3 | 0.3 | 0.3 |
| **Bucket 1 total** | **~12.2** | **~12.9** | **~12.3** |
| **SPV 80% (of increment over ~$2M baseline)** | **~$8.2M** | **~$8.7M** | **~$8.2M** |

¹ Wells Fargo Center (19k) — Philly is the #4 media market, Big 5 draw.
² Spectrum Center Charlotte (19k) — **and Davidson is Steph Curry's alma mater;
the anchor celebrity is baked in.**
³ Hawaii needs less relocation — Stan Sheriff is already 10,300, so the home gate
carries it; Diamond Head Classic supplies the marquee.

**Each reaches ~$12M a different way** — St. Joe's/Davidson by moving marquee games
to 19k NBA arenas, Hawaii by already having a 10k building. 80% lands at $8–9M every time.

## Per-school-season summary

- **Bucket 2 (~$15M)** recoups the $15M SPV cost.
- **Bucket 1 80% (~$8–10M)** is the investor return.
- **Plus** any Bucket 2 surplus above $15M (series margin + a large sponsor push it positive).
- **Donor track sits on top, not yet sized.**
