# D3 Making Cinderella — School × Celebrity Shortlist (with D1-move realism)
_Session: 2026-07-28 · companion to the D3 feasibility memo. Ties tagged **VERIFIED ALUM** /
**REGIONAL** / **PARTIAL** (attended, didn't graduate). Every tie source-checked (standing
rule); two of my first-pass assumptions were **wrong** and cut (see footnote)._

## The core finding
**Celebrity access at D3 is abundant; D1-willingness is the scarce ingredient.** Almost every
celebrity-rich D3 is an elite academic (NESCAC / UAA / NCAC / SCIAC) whose **D3 status is core
identity** — they will not move. And the entire D3→D1 category is **frozen by moratorium until
Feb 2027** regardless. So the clean "D3 school already moving to D1 + A-list tie" overlap
**does not exist today.**

**Workable strategy:** go **celebrity-first** and make the **D1 leap the show's central
quest** — the celebrity + Cinderella capital as the catalyst that convinces the school to go
for it. That's a better inciting incident than filming a move already decided. Trade-off: it's
a bigger lift (you're changing the school's trajectory, not documenting one).

### 1. NYU × Billy Crystal — wattage/market max
- **Tie:** Crystal = VERIFIED NYU film-school alum (Scorsese was his professor) **and a Basketball
  Hall of Fame "superfan"** (honored 2024 w/ Spike Lee) — courtside Clippers fixture since '85.
  A basketball-obsessed A-lister at his own alma mater: bullseye. Alts: **Adam Sandler**
  (verified alum, already made *Hustle*; currently slotted UNH), **Lady Gaga** (attended Tisch).
- **Buried hook that changes the pitch:** **NYU was a NYC basketball *power* in the 1940s-60s**,
  playing at Madison Square Garden (Final Fours, NIT) before de-emphasizing. That makes this a
  **"return to glory"** arc, not just a climb — the strongest story of the group, in the #1 media
  market, with MSG as a ready marquee venue.
- **Realism:** Low — NYU is a huge, committed-D3 institution. But that tension *is* the premise,
  and the history + market + wattage make it the most valuable **concept** even if the move is
  the hardest sell.

### 2. Ithaca × Bob Iger — strategic/industry max, and the most plausible mover
- **Tie:** Iger = VERIFIED Ithaca '73 (Park School, TV-radio), major donor, honorary degree.
- **Why it's different:** he's a **media-industrial** tie, not talent — **ex-Disney chairman =
  the single most useful person alive for a streaming/ESPN/distribution deal.** Public wattage
  moderate; industry leverage off the charts.
- **Realism: the best on the list.** Ithaca is a mid-size private with a pre-professional comms
  culture and no NESCAC-style anti-D1 ideology — a national-brand D1 move is *conceivable*, and
  Iger's involvement would be transformative. **Caveat:** would Iger front a basketball venture?
  Big ask — but the Disney/ESPN alignment is real and unmatched.

### 3. WashU St. Louis × Andy Cohen — celebrity-first
- **Tie:** REGIONAL — Cohen is a **St. Louis native (Clayton), deep WashU family lineage, and
  gave WashU's 2026 commencement** — but he's a **BU grad, NOT a WashU alum.** Big personality,
  Bravo/production machine, real sports fan (Blues/Cardinals).
- **Realism:** Low — WashU is a UAA academic purist. Good wattage/market, pure story play.

### 4. Occidental × Barack Obama — novelty max
- **Tie:** PARTIAL — Obama's **first two undergrad years ('79-'81)** before transferring to

- **Realism:** Low (SCIAC purist) and Obama is already slotted UIC/DePaul. Novelty high,

### 5-6. Wesleyan × Lin-Manuel Miranda / Kenyon × Allison Janney — right star, wrong tier
- Both **VERIFIED alums** with A-list wattage, but **NESCAC/NCAC hold the hardest anti-D1
  stance** — move realism ~zero. File as "celebrity yes, D1 no."

## The other axis — genuine D3→D1 aspirants (feasibility-first)
The schools that would actually *make the move* are ambitious mid-size privates chasing a
national brand — and they typically **lack famous alumni**, so you'd attach a strong **REGIONAL**
celebrity instead. Recent real movers skew **D2→D1** (Le Moyne, West Georgia); direct D3→D1 is
rare, and the category is **frozen to Feb 2027**. (Counter-signal worth noting: **Saint Francis
(PA) is going the *other* way, D1→D3** for 2026-27 — evidence the D1 cost curve is real.)
**Action:** when the moratorium lifts, run a scan for D3 schools with live D1 feasibility studies
+ a viable regional celebrity — that's where the near-term *feasible* version lives.

## Recommendation
- **No clean overlap exists — pick the axis:**
  - **Content/wattage-max → NYU** (MSG "return-to-glory" history + NYC + Crystal/Sandler/Gaga).
    Accept that the D1 move is the series' central quest.
  - **Strategic-max → Ithaca + Iger** — most plausible mover *and* the best media-deal partner
    alive. My pick if the goal is a real, buildable deal rather than a sizzle concept.
- **Feasible near-term → feasibility-first:** an ambitious mid-size private already eyeing the
  move + a regional celebrity (research post-Feb-2027).
- **Sequence after the D1 flagships** (Davidson / St. Joe's) prove the format regardless.
