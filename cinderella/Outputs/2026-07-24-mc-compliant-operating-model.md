## The one rule everything hangs on — the abstraction gradient

> **MC shapes the PHILOSOPHY and the ENVIRONMENT. MC never touches the specific
> PROSPECT or makes the specific PROMISE. Abstract in, specific out.**

| Activity | Who | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "What player DNA / positions win in March" (team philosophy) | MC / celebrity / Norman | ✅ permissible |
| "This program has a national docuseries + real NIL + pro mentorship" | MC / celebrity | ✅ environment |
| MC's FMV comp *structure* exists (featured "main characters" earn more) | MC | ✅ legitimate |
| Mentoring / developing **enrolled** players | Norman / celebrity | ✅ permissible |
| Opinion on which **unsigned** prospect to pursue | anyone at MC | ❌ recruiting participation |
| MC or celebrity quoting a **recruit** a pay number/range | anyone at MC | ❌ inducement |
| School staff quoting a recruit the **school rev-share** number | coach | ✅ school recruiting w/ its own money |

Everything MC does that's valuable — the eye, the philosophy, the platform, the
mentorship — lives on the **abstract/environment** side. The two things MC can never do
— evaluate specific recruits, promise specific money — live on the **specific** side,
and the **school owns both of those**.

## Who does what (roles)

| Function | School staff (countable coaches) | Norman / MC | Celebrity | NIL/comp administrator (≠ Norman) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruit / contact prospects | ✅ only they may | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Evaluate specific unsigned prospects | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Decide roster / who plays | ✅ | ❌ (funds *budget*, not spots) | ❌ | ❌ |
| Quote comp to recruits | ✅ (rev-share firm; NIL general) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Team-building *philosophy* (archetypes) | ✅ | ✅ abstract only | ✅ abstract only | — |
| Mentor / develop **enrolled** players | ✅ | ✅ sanctioned/disclosed | ✅ | — |
| Build evaluation system / IP | — | ✅ | — | — |
| Set / administer NIL pricing | — | ❌ (walled off) | ❌ | ✅ |
| On-camera evaluator / storyteller | — | ✅ (dev/futures, not pricing) | ✅ (face/story) | — |

**Norman's firewall (corrected framing):** the evaluator-vs-checkbook split is an
**institutional-control + conduct** measure — it keeps MC from directing the *school's*
roster and keeps Norman's conduct clean. It does **NOT** insulate Norman from being a
principal/booster of the entity paying players — he is one, and that's *permitted*, so
the defense rests on clean FMV payments + MC independence + school control, not on the
firewall.

## The money map (three streams that must not touch)

| Stream | Flow | Sized by | Recruit-facing? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. School rev-share** (institutional, ≤$20.5M cap; ~$3M to MBB) | school → players | school's cap allocation | ✅ **coach quotes it** — this is the clean recruiting number |
| **2. Third-party NIL** (uncapped) | MC/SPV → **enrolled** players | **FMV** of real services (docuseries, sponsor, merch, likeness); cleared via NIL Go | ❌ never promised to a recruit; general "opportunity exists here" only |
| **3. Commercial rev-share + conf. fee** | school → MC | **commercial revenue** (80% above baseline); services fee, not media-rights | n/a — MC's return, not player comp |

- Stream 2 **stacks on top of** Stream 1 (the school's ~$3M), not through it → ~$15M total player comp, ACC-competitive.
- Stream 3 must be **independent** of roster spend: never calibrated to, invoiced as, or
  described as "backfill/reimbursement" of player pay. It becomes **enterprise profit**,
  redeployed across the portfolio / next cohort — **no dollar of a school's rev-share is
  earmarked to its own players** (breaks any pass-through/circumvention claim).

## What gets said to a recruit

- **Coach:** "Your revenue share from us is $X [firm], and players here have had NIL
  opportunities worth around $Y [general, not a guaranteed booster promise]."
- **MC / celebrity:** describe the **environment** only — the platform, the docuseries,
  the mentorship, real NIL infrastructure. **No numbers, no promises, no specific
  prospect talk.**
- **No one** guarantees a specific third-party figure before the athlete signs.

---

## Celebrity lanes (identical booster status if paid by MC / owns SPV equity)

**Do:** be the face/story/culture; mentor **enrolled** players; on-campus presence during
visits **run by compliance**; general social-media halo; legit NIL deals with enrolled
players.
**Don't:** call or contact recruits; opine on specific unsigned prospects; get flown to a
recruit off-campus / used as a closer; promise money.
*(Recruiting was never the celebrity's value — this costs nothing.)*

---

## Red lines (never, regardless of framing)

1. No MC/celebrity **recruiting contact, calls, or evaluation** of unsigned prospects —
   even if the staff shares its target list first (direction doesn't cleanse it).
2. No MC/celebrity **quoting a recruit** money.
3. No **calibrated** school→MC payment that tracks roster spend ("backfill").
4. No **Norman school-employment** at an MC school while he holds MC equity; no employment
   at **two** MC schools at once.
5. No **cross-school player steering** between MC schools (SPV information walls).
6. No **on-screen depiction** of Norman pricing players or directing the roster/pay.
7. No **benefits** to players beyond sanctioned expertise/time + genuine FMV NIL.
8. MC must **not be an "associated entity"** of any school — independent commercial
   enterprise, documented as such.
