run through Clerky/Carta/Atlas or a flat-fee lawyer before signing/filing._

**Everything here must be done BEFORE the Aug 15 seed round (run by Sunjay/Ankur).**
The whole point is to fix the 83(b), cure the chain, and lock in your control while
you are still the **sole stockholder** — after outside stock exists, every one of
these gets harder or needs others' consent.

## Critical sequencing note
- **#1–#4 (83(b) cure) can run in parallel with #5** but must all be done first
  and the 83(b) **mailed** before the round. Get a startup CPA to bless the FMV
  (#1) this week — it's the one time-sensitive external dependency.
- **#6–#8 (dual-class) must happen while you're the sole stockholder.** Do NOT
  issue any advisor/investor stock (Sunjay, Ankur, Greg) until the A&R is filed
  and your 3M is reissued as Class B.
- **#9 (Voting Agreement)** you sign now as the only holder; every new holder
  signs as a condition of getting stock. Hand the executed version to Sunjay to
  fold into the round docs.

## Two ⚠️ items — do NOT free-hand
- **`02` new Founder SPA** and **`05` A&R Certificate**: use Clerky/Carta/Atlas
  (all support dual-class + restricted founder stock) or a flat-fee lawyer for
  these two. The drafts here are to show you exactly what they must contain and
  to hand to the platform/lawyer so you're not paying them to think — just to
  paper. Everything else you execute yourself.

The old Founder SPA / 83(b) had your SSN on it. The `03` template here is
**blank** on that field on purpose. Only write it on the physical copy you mail
