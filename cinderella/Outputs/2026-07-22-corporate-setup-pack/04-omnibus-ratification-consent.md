# DOCUMENT 04 — Omnibus Ratification (sole director + sole stockholder)
_DRAFT · cures any defect in the early corporate acts before you build on top._

**OMNIBUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR AND SOLE STOCKHOLDER OF
CINDERELLA CORP.**

The undersigned, being both the sole director and the sole stockholder of
Cinderella Corp., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), acting by written
consent in lieu of a meeting pursuant to Sections 141(f) and 228 of the Delaware
General Corporation Law, hereby ratifies, adopts, and approves in all respects
each action taken by or on behalf of the Company since its incorporation on
April 13, 2026, including without limitation:

(a) the filing of the Certificate of Incorporation (File No. 10581972) and the
    appointment and subsequent resignation of the incorporator, Nat Smith of
    Northwest Registered Agent, and the incorporator's appointment of Norman C.
    de Silva as director and officer;

(b) the adoption of the Company's Bylaws;

(c) the election of Norman C. de Silva as sole director and his appointment as
    President, Treasurer, and Secretary;

(d) the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement dated April 14, 2026;

(e) the original issuance of 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Norman C. de
    Silva, and the subsequent repurchase, cancellation, and re-grant of such
    shares pursuant to the resolutions of even or prior date (Docs 01–02);

(f) the obtaining of the Company's EIN and the opening of any bank accounts; and

(g) all other actions taken in furtherance of organizing the Company.

Each such action is hereby ratified and confirmed as the valid act of the
Company, effective as of the date such action was originally taken, pursuant to
DGCL §§ 204–205 to the extent any such action was defective.

Effective as of [DATE], 2026.

`__________________________`
Norman C. de Silva, **Sole Director**

`__________________________`
Norman C. de Silva, **Sole Stockholder**
