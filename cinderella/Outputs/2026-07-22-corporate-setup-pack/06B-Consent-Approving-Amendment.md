**JOINT WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR AND SOLE STOCKHOLDER OF CINDERELLA CORP.**

The undersigned, being the sole director and the sole stockholder of Cinderella Corp., a
Delaware corporation (the "Company"), acting by written consent pursuant to Sections
141(f), 228, and 242 of the Delaware General Corporation Law, adopts the following
resolutions, effective ____________, 2026:

**1. Approval of the Certificate of Amendment.**
RESOLVED, that the Certificate of Amendment of the Company's Certificate of Incorporation
in substantially the form attached (Doc 11B) — amending Article FOURTH to create Class A
Common Stock (one vote per share) and Class B Common Stock (ten votes per share) and to
reclassify the outstanding shares as Class B Common Stock, and adding director exculpation
(and indemnification) — is approved and adopted in all respects, and the officers are
authorized to execute and file it with the Delaware Division of Corporations.

**2. Reclassification of Founder Shares to Class B.**
RESOLVED, that upon effectiveness of the Certificate of Amendment, the 3,000,000 shares
held by Norman C. de Silva are reclassified as 3,000,000 shares of Class B Common Stock,
with the same vesting, repurchase, and transfer-restriction terms; the prior certificate
is cancelled and a new Class B certificate (Doc 09) is issued; and the stock ledger
(Doc 10) is updated accordingly.

**3. Board Size Fixed at Three (3).**
RESOLVED, that the number of directors is fixed at three (3); that Norman C. de Silva
occupies Seat 1; that Seats 2 and 3 remain vacant until filled by the Board; and that the
board size shall not change except with the written consent of Norman C. de Silva for so
long as he holds any Class B Common Stock.

**4. Future Issuances Are Class A.**
RESOLVED, that all shares issued after the date hereof shall be Class A Common Stock unless
the Board expressly resolves otherwise.

**5. General Authority.**
RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to take all actions and execute and file all
documents necessary to effect the foregoing.

Effective as of the date first written above.

`__________________________`  Norman C. de Silva, **Sole Director**

`__________________________`  Norman C. de Silva, **Sole Stockholder**
