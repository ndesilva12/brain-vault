### ARTICLE FOURTH — Capital Stock

**A. Authorized Shares.** The total number of shares the Corporation is
authorized to issue is **[20,000,000]** shares, consisting of:
- **[15,000,000]** shares of **Class A Common Stock**, par value $0.001; and
- **[5,000,000]** shares of **Class B Common Stock**, par value $0.001.

> _Note: bump the authorized count now (e.g., 20M) so there's headroom for the
> option pool and the seed round without another amendment. Keep Class B capped
> at ~5M — you only need 3M — so the super-voting bloc can never balloon._

**B. Identical Rights Except Voting.** Except as to voting and conversion set
forth below, the Class A and Class B Common Stock are **identical in all
respects** and have equal rights, powers, and privileges — including equal
per-share rights to dividends and to distributions on liquidation.

**C. Voting.**
1. Each share of **Class A Common Stock** entitles the holder to **one (1) vote**
   per share on all matters submitted to a vote of stockholders.
2. Each share of **Class B Common Stock** entitles the holder to **ten (10)
   votes** per share on all matters submitted to a vote of stockholders.
3. Except as otherwise required by law or this Certificate, Class A and Class B
   vote together as a single class.

**D. Automatic Conversion of Class B.**
1. Each share of Class B Common Stock shall **automatically convert** into one
   fully paid share of Class A Common Stock upon any **sale, assignment,
   transfer, or other disposition**, whether voluntary or involuntary, of such
   share or of any legal or beneficial interest therein, **other than a Permitted
   Transfer**.
2. **"Permitted Transfer"** means a transfer by Norman C. de Silva to (a) a
   revocable living trust or other estate-planning vehicle of which he is the
   trustee and retains sole voting control, or (b) an entity wholly owned and
   controlled by him, in each case provided he retains exclusive voting power
   over the shares. Any transfer that does not meet this definition triggers
   automatic conversion.
3. **[OMITTED by design — do NOT include now:]** service-termination conversion
   and death conversion. These are deliberately left out to keep the structure
   at maximum founder strength; they are the first concessions to offer at
   seed/Series A.
4. Shares of Class B that convert to Class A are retired and may not be reissued
   as Class B.

**E. Protective Vote for Class B.** The Corporation shall not, without the
affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the outstanding Class B Common
Stock, amend, alter, or repeal any provision of this Certificate in a manner
that adversely affects the rights, powers, or privileges of the Class B Common
Stock.

### ARTICLE [FIFTH] — Limitation of Director Liability (DGCL §102(b)(7))

To the fullest extent permitted by the Delaware General Corporation Law as it
now exists or may hereafter be amended, **no director of the Corporation shall be
personally liable to the Corporation or its stockholders for monetary damages
for breach of fiduciary duty as a director.** Any amendment, repeal, or
modification of this Article shall be prospective only and shall not adversely
affect any right or protection of a director existing at the time of such
amendment, repeal, or modification.

> _This MUST live in the certificate, not the bylaws — the statute only shields
> you if it's in the charter. Your filed April cert doesn't have it; this fixes
> the gap._

---

### ARTICLE [SIXTH] — Indemnification (DGCL §145)

The Corporation shall indemnify, and advance expenses to, its directors and
officers to the fullest extent permitted by the Delaware General Corporation
Law. The Corporation may purchase and maintain insurance on behalf of any
director, officer, employee, or agent. Any amendment or repeal of this Article
shall not adversely affect any right of any person existing at the time of such
amendment or repeal with respect to acts or omissions occurring prior thereto.
