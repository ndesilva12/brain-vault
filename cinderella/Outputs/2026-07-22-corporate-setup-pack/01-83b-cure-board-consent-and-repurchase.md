# DOCUMENT 01 — 83(b) CURE: Board Consent (FMV + Repurchase) & Stock Cancellation
_DRAFT · Cinderella Corp., a Delaware corporation · review before signing_

> **What this does:** Authorizes the Company to repurchase all 3,000,000 unvested
> founder shares at their original $0.001 price and cancel them, so a fresh
> restricted grant (Doc 02) can be made and a timely 83(b) filed (Doc 03). Because
> the shares are still 100% unvested and subject to repurchase at cost, and there
> has been no financing, liquidity event, or material change, the repurchase is at
> the same nominal price paid — no gain, no gift, nothing to tax.

---

## PART A — WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR (in lieu of a meeting)

**ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CINDERELLA CORP.**

The undersigned, being the sole director of Cinderella Corp., a Delaware
corporation (the "Company"), acting by written consent in lieu of a meeting
pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law and the
Company's Bylaws, hereby adopts the following resolutions, effective as of
[DATE], 2026:

**1. Fair-Market-Value Determination.**
RESOLVED, that the Board, having reviewed the Company's current condition —
including that the Company has (i) conducted no financing, (ii) had no liquidity
event, (iii) generated no revenue, and (iv) undergone no material change in its
business since incorporation on April 13, 2026 — determines that the fair market
value of the Company's Common Stock as of the date hereof is $0.001 per share,
consistent with the price at which the founder shares were originally issued;
and RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company is authorized to obtain a written
valuation (409A or CPA memorandum) confirming such value for its records.

**2. Repurchase and Cancellation of Unvested Founder Shares.**
WHEREAS, on or about April 14, 2026, the Company issued 3,000,000 shares of
Common Stock to Norman C. de Silva (the "Founder") under a Founder Stock
Purchase and Restriction Agreement (the "Original SPA"), all of which shares
remain unvested and subject to the Company's repurchase right at the original
purchase price, the one-year cliff not occurring until April 14, 2027; and

WHEREAS, the Company and the Founder wish to unwind the Original grant and
replace it with a new restricted grant on substantially identical terms to
enable a timely election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code;

RESOLVED, that the Company is authorized to repurchase and cancel all 3,000,000
unvested shares held by the Founder at the original purchase price of $0.001 per
share (aggregate $3,000.00), such repurchase price to be satisfied by offset,
cancellation of the corresponding subscription obligation, or refund as the
officers determine; and

RESOLVED FURTHER, that upon such repurchase the 3,000,000 shares shall be
cancelled and returned to the status of authorized but unissued Common Stock,
and the Original SPA shall be terminated in its entirety.

**3. Authorization of New Founder Grant.**
RESOLVED, that the Company is authorized to issue 3,000,000 shares of Common
Stock to the Founder under a new Founder Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement
(Doc 02) at $0.001 per share, on vesting and repurchase terms substantially
identical to the Original SPA except that the vesting commencement date shall be
the date of the new grant; and RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers are directed
to advise the Founder to file an election under Section 83(b) within thirty (30)
days of the new grant.

**4. Confirmation of IP Assignment.**
RESOLVED, that the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement dated April 14,
2026, under which the Founder assigned all "Making Cinderella" intellectual
property to the Company, remains in full force and effect and is unaffected by
the repurchase and re-grant; the consideration for the new grant is the
Founder's continued service and the previously assigned IP, and no additional
consideration is owed.

**5. General Authority.**
RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to take all actions and execute all
documents necessary to carry out the foregoing.

Effective as of the date first written above.

`__________________________`
**Norman C. de Silva, Sole Director**
Date: ____________

## PART B — STOCK REPURCHASE & CANCELLATION ACKNOWLEDGMENT

**Cinderella Corp. — Repurchase & Cancellation of Restricted Stock**

Reference is made to the Founder Stock Purchase and Restriction Agreement dated
April 14, 2026 (the "Original SPA") between Cinderella Corp. (the "Company") and
Norman C. de Silva (the "Founder").

1. The Founder and the Company agree that all 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock
   issued under the Original SPA remain **fully unvested** and subject to the
   Company's repurchase right at the original price.

2. Effective [DATE], the Company exercises its repurchase right and repurchases
   all 3,000,000 shares at $0.001 per share (aggregate $3,000.00), satisfied by
   offset/cancellation of the original subscription amount. The Founder receives
   no gain.

3. The 3,000,000 shares are **cancelled** and returned to authorized-but-unissued
   status. The Original SPA is **terminated**. Any stock certificate previously
   issued is void; the Founder shall return or the Company shall mark it
   cancelled in the stock ledger.

4. The parties acknowledge this repurchase is undertaken solely to permit a
   contemporaneous re-grant (Doc 02) and a timely 83(b) election, and does not
   reflect any change in the Founder's role, economics, or the assigned IP.

> ⚠️ **CPA sign-off before you execute:** have a startup CPA confirm the $0.001
> FMV in writing (a short 409A/valuation memo) and confirm the repurchase-at-cost
> creates no taxable event. This is the one item to get blessed externally, and
> it's fast/cheap. Then execute Part A, Part B, Doc 02, and Doc 03 **on the same
> day** so the grant date and 83(b) clock are clean.
