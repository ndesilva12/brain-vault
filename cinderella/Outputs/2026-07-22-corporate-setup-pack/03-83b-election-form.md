> **How to file (do all of this within 30 days of the Doc 02 grant date):**
> 1. Print two copies. Handwrite your SSN and sign/date both.
> 2. Mail one to the **IRS Service Center where you file your personal return**
>    (for MA residents filing a 1040, that's the address in the current 1040
>    instructions — verify the current address; the IRS changes them).
> 3. Send it **USPS Certified Mail, Return Receipt Requested** — the green card +
>    certified receipt is your proof of timely filing. Keep it forever.
> 4. Keep the second signed copy in the corporate record book.
> 5. (The requirement to attach a copy to your tax return was repealed, but keep
>    your proof.) Give a copy to your CPA.

> **Why the timing matters (the whole reason for the cure):** income under 83(b)
> = FMV − price. Today FMV = price = $0.001, so income = **$0.00**. If you did
> this *after* the Aug 15 round set FMV at ~$2/share, income would be
> ~(2 − 0.001) × 3,000,000 ≈ **$6M of ordinary income**. That is the cliff you're
> beating. File before Aug 15.
