**VOTING AGREEMENT**

This Voting Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of [DATE], 2026 by and among
**Cinderella Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), **Norman C. de
Silva** (the "Founder"), and each other person who executes a counterpart or
joinder hereto (each a "Stockholder"). Enforceable under DGCL § 218(c).

### 1. Board Composition
Each Stockholder agrees to vote all shares it holds, at every election of
directors, so that:
(a) the board consists of **three (3) directors**;
(b) **two (2)** directors are designated by the Founder (the "Founder
    Directors"); the Founder is one of them;
(c) **one (1)** director is designated by the lead investor in the Company's
    seed financing (the "Investor Director") — anticipated to be **Ankur Jain**
    at the Aug 15, 2026 close; until so designated the seat remains a Founder
    vacancy;
(d) the person entitled to designate a director is the only person entitled to
    remove and replace that director; and
(e) the board size shall not change except with the Founder's written consent.

### 2. Irrevocable Founder Proxy (backstop)
Each Stockholder hereby grants to the Founder an **irrevocable proxy**, coupled
with an interest, to vote all of such Stockholder's shares on (i) the election
and removal of directors and (ii) any matter as to which such Stockholder has
agreed to vote under this Agreement, in each case in accordance with this
Agreement. This proxy is irrevocable for the term of this Agreement and survives
transfer of the shares (a transferee takes subject to it). *This is the
maximum-strength backstop; §7 lets it be amended/released as investors negotiate.*

### 3. Drag-Along
If the Founder and the board approve a sale of the Company (stock sale, merger,
or sale of substantially all assets), each Stockholder agrees to (a) vote all
shares in favor, (b) not exercise appraisal/dissenters' rights, and (c) execute
the sale documents on the same terms (subject to customary limitations: several
not joint liability, cap at proceeds, no non-competes forced on minority
holders). *Founder-controlled — cleans up a future exit.*

### 4. Founder / CEO Protection
No Stockholder shall vote to (a) remove the Founder as President/CEO, (b) expand
or contract the board, or (c) amend this Agreement, in each case without the
Founder's written consent, for so long as the Founder holds Class B Common Stock.

### 5. Legend & Joinder
Certificates bear a legend noting this Agreement. Receipt of Company stock is
conditioned on executing a joinder — no one gets shares without signing.

### 6. Term
Until the earliest of (a) a Qualified IPO, (b) a sale of the Company, or (c)
written agreement of the Founder and holders of a majority of the shares then
subject hereto.

### 7. Amendment
This Agreement may be amended or any provision waived only with the written
consent of the Company, the Founder, and holders of a majority of the shares
then subject to this Agreement — **provided that** no amendment adverse to the
Founder's rights under §§ 1, 2, or 4 is effective without the Founder's written
consent. *This is the "strongest now, work backwards" lever: nothing here weakens
without you signing, but the clause exists so you CAN trade pieces at seed/Series
A when you choose to.*

### 8. Miscellaneous
Delaware law; **specific performance** available (voting agreements are specifically
enforceable under DGCL §218(c)); counterparts; entire agreement on its subject.
