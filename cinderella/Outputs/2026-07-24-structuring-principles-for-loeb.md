# Cinderella Corp — NIL / Rev-Share Structuring Principles
### For counsel review (Loeb & Loeb — Brian Socolow)
_Prepared 2026-07-24 · a structuring position to pressure-test, not a settled plan._

**Context.** Cinderella Corp ("MC") funds third-party NIL for a school's basketball
roster (via a per-school SPV) while separately earning a commercial revenue share from
operating that school's commercial rights and producing a documentary series. We want
your read on whether the principles below hold up against (a) NCAA institutional-control
and tampering rules and (b) the House settlement's cap-circumvention / "associated
entity" enforcement by the College Sports Commission. Where they don't, we want the
edits that make them hold.

---

## A. Independence (MC is not an "associated entity")
1. MC is an **independent commercial enterprise** — its own IP, franchise, and
   documentary business — **not controlled by, and not a booster vehicle of, any school.**
2. No school appoints, directs, or holds equity giving control over MC or any SPV.
3. MC's capital is **mixed-source at the parent level** (not single-school donor money).
4. MC documents its commercial substance (productions, sponsors, merch, media rights)
   contemporaneously, anticipating a possible "associated entity" challenge.

## B. Institutional control (the school runs its team)
5. MC sets an **NIL budget** and makes NIL **deals available**; it does **not** select
   the roster.
6. The **coach/AD decide** recruiting and playing time; the **player decides** whether to
   sign. MC funds *opportunity*, not *roster spots*.
7. Decision authority is papered so the school can decline any MC-funded deal without
   penalty.

## C. No tampering / no inducement
8. MC engages **only players already enrolled or committed** to the contracted school.
9. **No cross-school steering** — MC never moves a player between MC schools; SPVs
   operate at arm's length behind information walls.

## D. Two independent streams (the anti-circumvention core)
10. **Stream A — MC → players:** third-party NIL for **genuine services** (documentary
    appearances, sponsor activations, merchandise, likeness), priced at **fair market
    value**, clearing NIL Go on its own merits. Funded from **production + sponsor +
    MC capital.**
11. **Stream B — school → MC:** a **commercial return** for MC operating the school's
    commercial engine, **benchmarked to commercial revenue** (e.g., 80% of revenue above
    a fixed baseline), **not to roster cost.**
12. The two streams are **not contingent** on each other; Stream B is **never sized by,
    invoiced as, or described as reimbursement of** player payments. The words "backfill"
    and "reimbursement" appear nowhere.
13. **Timing:** the roster is funded up front (production/sponsor/capital) **before** any
    school rev-share is due; rev-share arrives after revenue materializes and is
    demonstrably not a roster input.

## E. Enterprise fungibility (breaks the pass-through)
14. School rev-share is **enterprise profit**, redeployed across the portfolio (other
    SPVs / next cohort) and to investors — **no dollar of a school's rev-share is
    earmarked to its own players.**
15. MC operates as a genuine multi-school business; no 1:1 mapping exists between any
    school's rev-share and its own roster spend.

## F. Conference success fee (BC-specific)
16. Any fee indexed to a school's conference (ACC) distribution is a **services fee paid
    from the school's general funds**, **not** an assignment of NCAA/ACC media rights.

## G. Norman's role — evaluator/mentor, not checkbook, employee, or recruiter
17. **Owner stays external.** While Norman holds an MC economic interest, he does **not**
    take a school-employee role at any MC school (that would put him on both sides of the
    MC↔school transaction and re-collapse institutional control). He operates at the
    **MC/parent level**, external and even-handed across schools.
18. **Not employed by multiple MC schools.** Norman is not a consultant/employee of two
    or more MC schools simultaneously (divided loyalty between competitors; breaks the
    anti-steering wall; undermines MC independence). Title (consultant vs GM) is irrelevant.
19. **Evaluator/checkbook separation.** The person who evaluates talent and the person who
    administers NIL money are not the same head in the same room; Norman is walled off from
    comp-setting/pricing.
20. **Mentorship is permitted, bounded.** Norman may serve as a **sanctioned, disclosed**
    basketball advisor/mentor to **enrolled** student-athletes — game, mindset, NBA/draft
    development, performance — providing **expertise and time, not benefits**. He does not:
    (a) contact not-yet-committed prospects (booster/rep restriction); (b) act as players'
    agent for compensation; (c) tie marketability guidance to specific comp or use it as an
    inducement.

## H. Documentary depiction firewall
21. **On-screen role = evaluator / storyteller / mentor** (talent, NBA projection,
    development, futures).
22. **Never depicted:** pricing players in a way tied to pay, directing the staff's roster
    or pay decisions, or any compensation mechanism.
23. **MC reserves approval** over how Norman's role is portrayed; **compliance counsel reads
    the cut** before release (the edit must match the legal structure).

---

### Questions for counsel
1. Do Principles D + E defeat a cap-circumvention / associated-entity claim, or does the
   commercial rev-share (Stream B) itself risk re-characterization as disguised roster
   funding? What contract language best insulates it?
2. What contemporaneous documentation best establishes MC's **independence** (Principle A)
   against the CSC's current aggressive "associated entity" posture?
3. Is the **budget-not-roster** framing (B) sufficient for institutional control, or do we
   need specific decision-rights language in the school agreement?
4. Does the **multi-school** structure create tampering/antitrust exposure beyond
   single-school, and how do the information walls (C, Principle 9) need to be papered?
5. Is the conference **services fee** (F) clean under ACC bylaws and NCAA rules?
6. **Dual role (the crux):** given Norman **owns** MC, is any school-employee/consultant
   role at an MC school salvageable, or must he stay entirely external (G17–18)? What
   ownership/recusal facts would change the answer?
7. Where exactly is the line between permissible **mentorship** of enrolled players and an
   impermissible **extra benefit** or **inducement** (G20)? What sanctioning/disclosure by
   the school best protects it?
8. What contract terms secure MC's **approval over on-screen depiction** (H) and a
   compliance read of the cut, without EverWonder/streamer resistance killing it?

_All figures/structures illustrative. This is a position to test, not a representation of
current operations._
