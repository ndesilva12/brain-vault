## The $75K founder incentive — a recoverable origination advance

Structured as a **pre-payment against Norman's SPV origination fee**, not a
raise bonus:
- Cinderella Corp pays Norman a **$200K origination commission** each time he
  originates and closes a new SPV, funded from that SPV's budget.
- The parent **fronts $75K of the first SPV's origination fee now** from the
  raise, and **recovers it from that SPV's first income** at close.

Why it's clean: **(1)** tied to building SPVs, not the fundraise (which Ankur
ran); **(2)** recoverable by the parent — a timing advance, not a permanent
cost; **(3)** aligned — Norman only keeps it by actually closing the first SPV.
