# MAKING CINDERELLA

_A multi-season sports-entertainment franchise · Cinderella Corp._

_The Cinderella run — engineered. A celebrity, private capital, and an overlooked Division I basketball program, transformed into a national phenomenon and captured as a premium docuseries. Welcome to Wrexham for American college hoops — but a franchise, not a one-off._

## The Concept

Every March, one unknown team captures the entire country. **Making Cinderella** manufactures that magic on purpose: pair an authentic celebrity with a program that has a real story, inject the capital and roster talent to make it win, and film the transformation across a season — the town, the students, the coaches, the gambles, the run. A story about place, people, and purpose, told through basketball.

## Why Now

- **The rules finally allow it.** NIL and the House settlement make it legal, for the first time, to fund rosters through legitimate third-party deals.
- **Streamers are in a live-sports gold rush** — and college basketball's non-conference schedule is unclaimed live inventory.
- **The underdog format is proven** (Welcome to Wrexham, Last Chance U) — but no one owns the *franchise* version: multiple schools, authentic stars, and real March stakes built in.

## The Format — A Multi-School Season 1

- **A portfolio of programs, not one team** — so no single March result makes or breaks the season, and every episode carries a live stake.
- **Authentic pairings** — each school matched to a celebrity with a genuine tie (alumnus, hometown hero, adopted city). The bond is the story.
- **A season-long transformation arc** — the roster build, the climb up the standings, the rivalries, the run.
- **Live, ownable inventory** — marquee non-conference "money games" moved to neutral sites and packaged as live events.

## What Comes With It

- **A signed and growing slate of exclusive school LOIs** — ready to go.
- **An NBA-caliber basketball operating system** — team selection, roster and portal strategy, scheduling, in-season tournaments — the part a content company cannot do.
- **Compliance, solved** — an NIL and institutional-control framework built with lead counsel; no legal risk to the platform.
- **A premium production partner** attached to produce.
- **International upside** — an Asia / China distribution track already in motion.

## Why It Can't Just Be Copied

The idea isn't the asset — the *system* is: exclusive school relationships, the basketball operating capability, the compliance architecture, the celebrity pairings, and the franchise brand. A partner can license a turnkey franchise — or spend years trying to build a sports-operations arm it never wanted.

## The Offer

Cinderella Corp. brings the franchise, the schools, the operating system, and the talent pairings. **We are looking for the platform partner to bring it to the world** — a season license, live-event rights, and a multi-season franchise built together. Cinderella Corp. retains the format and franchise; the platform gets the show, the reach, and the live games.
