# MAKING CINDERELLA — Concept Sizzle Reel
### Script, AI-generation prompt kit, and OpenArt step-by-step
_Rev. 2026-08-08b — "band of the overlooked" story. Target runtime 2:30–2:45. Concept/tone teaser,
not represented as real footage._
**New to OpenArt? Jump to §7.**

---

## 0. Read this first (the story + guardrails)

- **The story:** Big money doesn't just beat small schools — it **buys their best players away**, every
  year. Making Cinderella flips that: the overlooked, the passed-over, the players who'd normally sell
  out to a blue blood one by one instead **team up at a small school and go beat the giants at their own
  game.** Same money the big schools use to *take* them now **keeps them together.** And not one
  Cinderella — **several, in the same season.**
- **Position it honestly.** Open on a 1.5s super: _"Concept visualization."_
- **Real branding — Davidson, Saint Joseph's, George Washington ONLY.** We hold LOIs, so their marks,
  colors, and arenas are fair game: **Davidson** (red & black), **Saint Joseph's** (crimson & gray,
  Hawks), **George Washington** (buff & blue). **Every other school stays generic** (solid-color
  uniforms, no logos, invented names).
- **No real player or celebrity faces.** Players are cast/anonymous — we do not deepfake real athletes,
  Steph, or any celeb target. The star names live in the deck, not the reel.
- **Consistency is everything.** Append the **Style Block (§3)** to every prompt.

## 1. Cost strategy

| Use | Engine | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Heroes: the opener, the build, the buzzer, the horn — native audio + max realism | Veo 3.1 Quality — 4 shots | Highest cost; save it |
| Single-subject b-roll + the quiet portraits | Veo 3.1 Fast/Lite — 8 shots | Cheaper Veo tier |
| Crowds, locker room, multi-team flashes, money-arena, montages | Kling — 13 shots | Cheapest for multi-person |

Six rules: (1) nail the prompt → fewer re-rolls (~2/shot); (2) generate ≈4–5s, extend in edit;
(3) image-to-video for the pricey shots (§4b); (4) reuse b-roll; (5) audio/music/VO in the edit;
(6) skip Quality Veo unless it's a "hero."

## 3. The Style Block (paste at the end of EVERY prompt)

> Cinematic documentary style, anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1, shot on Arri Alexa with vintage anamorphic
> lenses, subtle 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, naturalistic motivated lighting, high dynamic
> range, photoreal. Cast/anonymous subjects, no recognizable real athletes or celebrities.

**Palette modifiers:**
- **BEFORE** (beats 1–2): _add_ "desaturated cold steel-blue palette, overcast, melancholy, still air."
- **RISE** (beats 3–7): _add_ "warm amber and gold palette, golden-hour glow, volumetric light, energy."

**Branding note:** for the three LOI schools, _add_ the real identity — "Davidson Wildcats, red and black"
· "Saint Joseph's Hawks, crimson and gray" · "George Washington, buff and blue." All other schools:
_add_ "generic solid-color uniforms, no logos."

## 4. Shot list + copy-paste prompts

### BEAT 1 — What the money did (BEFORE)  ·  _VO: "March used to belong to the little guy…"_
**1 — Veo 3.1 Quality (audio) — 5s** _(HERO — opener/bookend)_
> Extreme slow motion, a worn leather basketball rolls to a stop on a cracked, sunlit outdoor asphalt
> court, a rusted chain net sways above, dust drifts through a low shaft of light, no people. Audio: one
> lonely bounce echo, faint wind.

**2 — Kling — 4s** _(the antagonist — big money)_
> A gleaming, sold-out blue-blood basketball mega-arena at night, cold blue light, a giant LED ring
> streaming sponsor logos and glowing numbers, overwhelming corporate scale, the crowd a faceless sea.

**3 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 5s** _(the theft, made personal) → cut to TEXT: "CINDERELLA IS DYING."_
> A young small-school basketball player alone in a dim locker room zips a duffel bag and pulls a plain
> powerhouse-program jersey over his head, then walks toward a bright doorway as the small gym goes dark
> behind him, melancholy.

### BEAT 2 — What if they didn't leave (transition)  ·  _VO: "But what if they stopped leaving?…"_
**4 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 5s** _(the reversal)_
> The same young player pauses in the gym doorway, silhouetted against the light, looks back over his
> shoulder at the empty court, and slowly sets the duffel bag down. A held, hopeful beat.

**5 — Kling — 5s** _(the band gathers)_
> A small-college locker room fills with new arrivals — a dozen overlooked players in mismatched old
> team gear carrying duffels, bumping fists and embracing, a brotherhood forming, warm side light.

**6 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 5s** _(the architects)_
> A well-dressed figure seen from behind stands with a coach at a tactics whiteboard covered in index
> cards and magnets, assembling a roster, a single desk lamp, night.

### BEAT 3 — Same money. New game. (RISE · multi-team)  ·  _VO: "…now keeps them together…"_
**7 — Kling — 5s** _(the unified roster)_
> A full team of players walks out of a stadium tunnel together in fresh matching uniforms, backlit by
> arena light, purposeful, shoulder to shoulder, low hero angle.

**8 — Kling — 5s** _(MULTIPLE CINDERELLAS — real branding)_
> Fast intercutting between three different college gyms and their players: a Davidson Wildcats team in
> red and black, a Saint Joseph's Hawks team in crimson and gray, and a George Washington team in buff
> and blue — three underdog programs rising at once, energy building.

**9 — Kling — 5s** _(the lights come back on)_
> Stadium floodlights snap on in sequence across a dark small arena, banks of light igniting one after
> another, lens flares blooming, the court emerging from black into brilliant glow.

### BEAT 4 — The mission (RISE · multi-team)  ·  _VO: "…beat the giants at their own game…"_
**10 — Veo 3.1 Quality (audio) — 5s** _(HERO)_
> Slow push-in on athletic sneakers cutting and squeaking across a polished hardwood court during a
> footwork drill, sweat droplets flying, early-morning gym light through high windows. Audio: rhythmic
> squeak of shoes, ball bounce, breath.

**11 — Kling — 5s** _(multi-team build montage)_
> Rapid intercut of anonymous players grinding through practice and weight-room work across several
> different-colored uniforms, chalk dust and steam in shafts of light, relentless intensity.

**12 — Kling — 5s**
> A team bus rolls down an empty rural highway at dusk, headlights on, rolling farmland and a
> pink-orange sky behind, low tracking shot alongside.

**13 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 4s**
> Macro close-up of an athlete's hands wrapping white tape around a wrist, then gripping a basketball
> hard, tendons flexing, warm side light.

**14 — Kling — 5s** _(banners rise across the slate — real branding)_
> Fresh championship-style banners being hoisted into the rafters of multiple gyms — Davidson red and
> black, Saint Joseph's crimson and gray, George Washington buff and blue — and courts being repainted,
> warm afternoon light.

### BEAT 5 — The untold ones (RISE)  ·  _VO: "…the families, the professors, the alumni…"_
**15 — Kling — 5s**
> Time-lapse of a small-college arena filling with fans, seats going from half-empty to packed, a wave
> of color and motion, warm arena glow.

**16 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 4s** _(the janitor)_
> An older janitor slowly sweeps the empty hardwood court at dawn, alone, long shadow, warm light through
> high windows, quiet dignity and routine.

**17 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 4s** _(the professor)_
> A professor writes on a chalkboard in a near-empty lecture hall, chalk dust drifting in a beam of
> morning light, seen from the back rows, quiet devotion.

**18 — Kling — 5s** _(the families)_
> A mother and two young children in packed arena stands, faces tight with hope then leaping up in joy;

### BEAT 6 — March (RISE · multi-team)  ·  _VO: "…a miracle stops being an accident."_
**19 — Kling — 5s**
> A massive tournament arena packed to the rafters, confetti cannons, generic-uniformed players
> mid-celebration, a bracket-style graphic motif in the LED ring, blinding lights.

**20 — Veo 3.1 Quality (audio) — 5s — the dagger** _(HERO)_
> Slow motion, an anonymous player rises for a long three-pointer at the buzzer, ball spinning off the
> fingertips, a blurred roaring crowd behind, golden light. Audio: swelling crowd, then a beat of silence

**21 — Veo 3.1 Quality (audio) — 5s — the horn** _(HERO)_
> Slow motion final buzzer, anonymous players collapse to the floor in ecstatic disbelief, the coach

**22 — Kling — 4s**
> A packed student section erupts and storms forward in pandemonium, bodies leaping, arms up, confetti

**23 — Veo 3.1 Fast — 5s** _(shared triumph across the slate)_
> Slow motion, players cut down the net and ride teammates' shoulders, intercut quickly between teams in

### BEAT 7 — Title / tag (RISE)

## 5. Assembly notes
- **Edit software:** CapCut (free, fast) or DaVinci Resolve. Color-match Veo↔Kling.
- **Cut rhythm:** 2.5s holds in beats 1–2, tightening to ~1s by beat 4, fast 0.5–0.75s cuts through the
  multi-team flashes and beats 5–6, then the held 3s+ bookend. Cut on the music's beat.
- **Multi-team feel:** whenever you can, hold a shot on one color, then hard-cut to the same action in
  another team's color — that repetition is what sells "several Cinderellas at once."
- **VO:** spare, low, intimate read. Leave air.
- **Text cards:** two only — "CINDERELLA IS DYING." and the title.

## 6. Do-not-ship checklist
- [ ] "Concept visualization" super at head
- [ ] Real marks limited to Davidson / Saint Joseph's / George Washington; all others generic
- [ ] Zero real athlete or celebrity faces
- [ ] Music licensed (or clearly flagged as temp)
- [ ] Runtime ≤ 2:45

## 7. OpenArt — step by step (do this)

_Verified against OpenArt's current flow, Aug 2026. Confirm live credit costs on the generate screen;
all models draw from one shared credit pool._

### A. Setup (once)
1. **openart.ai**, sign up / log in. New accounts get free starter credits.
2. **Get a paid plan for the real push** — the free tier caps clips/day on the top Veo tier.
3. **Three Veo tiers by cost:** **Lite ≈ 10 cr** (cheap b-roll), **Fast ≈ 20 cr** (mid), **Quality ≈
   100 cr** (the 4 hero shots). **Kling** = crowds/multi-team.

### B. Generate the STILLS first — for the Veo shots
1. Open the **Image** generator.
2. Paste a **Still prompt (§4b)** + **Style Block (§3)** + palette line (+ branding note for D/SJU/GW).
3. Aspect **16:9** (no 2.39:1 — crop in the edit).
4. Generate 2–4, download the best. Re-roll here, not in video.

### C. Animate — Image-to-Video (Veo 3.1)
1. **Video → Image-to-Video → Veo 3.1.**
2. **Upload the still** as the first frame.
3. Paste the matching **§4 motion prompt.**
4. **Audio ON** for the 4 heroes (1, 10, 20, 21), OFF otherwise · **1080p · 16:9 · 4–6s** · **Quality**
   for heroes, **Fast/Lite** otherwise.
5. Generate; re-roll once if close. Download.

### D. Kling shots — Text-to-Video (no still)
1. **Video → Text-to-Video → Kling.**
2. Paste the **§4 prompt** + **Style Block** + palette (+ branding note where relevant).
3. **16:9 · 5s.** Generate 1–2, keep the winner.

### E. Budget reality (ballpark, list credits)
- 4 heroes × 2 × 100 = **800** · 8 Veo-fast × 2 × ~15 = **~240** · 13 Kling × 2 × ~25 = **~650** · stills
  cheap. **First full pass ≈ 1,800–2,500 credits** with normal re-rolls.
- Buy a credit pack / plan sized to that; watch daily caps on the Quality tier.

### F. Assemble
CapCut / DaVinci Resolve → **crop every 16:9 clip to 2.39:1** → color-match → cut to the beat → VO +
licensed track → add the "Concept visualization" super and the title card. Export 1080p.

## 8. Voiceover (done outside OpenArt)

**Tool:** **ElevenLabs** (elevenlabs.io) — Text to Speech. Free/cheap tier covers this ~150-word script.
Pick a **low, warm, restrained male voice** (prestige-doc narrator, NOT booming trailer guy). Settings:
**Stability ~50, Style 0–15, Speaker Boost on.** Generate **line by line** for pause control; download
each as MP3 → place under the beats in the edit → **duck the music under the VO.**
- **Option:** clone your own voice in ElevenLabs so the narrator is *you* (fitting — you're the doc guy).
- **Final buyer cut:** a human trailer narrator on Voices.com / Voice123 (~$100–400) if you want it — but
  a good ElevenLabs read is buyer-grade.
- **Do NOT** use Veo's audio for narration (that's clip-synced ambient/dialogue, not a clean VO track).
