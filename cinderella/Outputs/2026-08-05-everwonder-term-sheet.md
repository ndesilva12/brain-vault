# TERM SHEET (non-binding) — Cinderella Corp ↔ EverWonder
_Production partnership deal points. Draft for discussion; **not legal advice** — Loeb (Socolow)
to paper. Purpose: fix the ownership/role split **before** EverWonder internally greenlights
production (Becca Gitlitz as director/showrunner), so the partnership is "great producer," not
"producer who owns the franchise." Anchored to the EverWonder risk map in `cinderella/CLAUDE.md`._

## 1. Parties & structure
- **Cinderella Corp** ("Company") — owner of the *Making Cinderella* franchise; principal.
- **EverWonder** ("Producer") — production services partner for designated seasons.
- Producer is engaged as a **production vendor** for the season(s) specified; this is **not** a
  sale, assignment, or co-ownership of the franchise.

## 2. IP & format ownership (non-negotiable)
- Company **owns and retains** all right, title, and interest in: the *Making Cinderella*
  format and format bible, the trademark/brand, the multi-school franchise concept and
  structure, the compliance/operating system, and all underlying/created materials.
- **"Created by: Norman de Silva / Cinderella Corp"** and franchise/format credit are retained
  by Company on all seasons and derivatives, in perpetuity.
- Producer acquires **no ownership** of the format, franchise, or brand — only the agreed
  production credit and the defined production compensation below.

## 3. Producer role & credit
- Producer provides production services for the designated season(s); **Becca Gitlitz** as
  director/showrunner (or as mutually agreed).
- Credits: Producer receives a customary production company credit; Becca receives
  director/showrunner credit. Company receives "created by" + franchise/EP credit.

## 4. Economics
- Producer compensation = a **defined production fee** (and, if agreed, a **capped production
  backend on the specific seasons Producer makes**). **Define exactly what any percentage is a
  percentage *of*** — production budget vs. gross vs. net are radically different; the casual
  "flat 10%" framing is not accepted until scoped in writing.
- Producer receives **no franchise-level equity or franchise backend** — franchise economics
  (format license, renewals, future seasons, spin-offs) belong to Company.
- Distribution: the Company/SPV collects the **distributor license fee**; Producer does **not**
  own or collect distribution rights.

## 5. Buyer relationship & go-to-market
- **Company is the principal** in all buyer (streamer/network) negotiations. Producer
  participates as production partner and may contribute its relationships, but does **not**
  independently own, control, or separately shop the buyer relationship.
- All buyer outreach runs through **one coordinated process** (single lead/quarterback);
  Producer will not approach buyers with the project except as part of that process.

## 6. Scope & exclusivity (keep the counterweight alive)
- Producer is engaged for **specified seasons/schools only.** Company **retains the right to
  engage other producers** (e.g., Words + Pictures / Connor Schell) for other seasons, schools,
  or franchise extensions. No blanket franchise-wide production exclusivity to Producer.
- Any first-look/first-negotiation right for Producer on future seasons, if granted, is **time-
  limited and non-exclusive**, and does not attach to the franchise IP.

## 7. Control of basketball operations & compliance
- Company (Norman) controls basketball operations, school relationships, celebrity/talent
  sourcing, and the NCAA/NIL/institutional-control compliance framework. Producer controls
  day-to-day physical production. Producer defers to Company + school approval rights
  (per school LOIs) on institutional portrayal.

## 8. Talent
- Talent (celebrity leads) are sourced/approved jointly and ultimately subject to buyer casting
  input; **no single talent is a condition** of the partnership. Producer does not control
  talent deals or own talent relationships beyond a given production.

## 9. Confidentiality & non-circumvention
- Mutual NDA + non-circumvention (Company's existing 5-yr / 3-yr non-circ template applies):
  Producer will not use, replicate, or pursue the concept, schools, talent, or format outside
  this partnership, or bring a competing basketball-transformation franchise to a buyer.

## 10. Term, approvals, termination
- Defined term per engaged season(s); Company approval over franchise-brand-affecting creative;
  standard termination/turnaround provisions; work-for-hire on production deliverables with
  **all franchise/format IP vesting in Company**.

## 11. Governing law / next step
- Governing law TBD (align with Cinderella Corp entity — Delaware/NY). Non-binding except any
  confidentiality/non-circ provisions the parties agree to make binding. **Loeb to convert to a
  definitive Production Services / Co-Production Agreement.**

---
_Bottom line the sheet protects: Company keeps the **franchise, format, brand, created-by,
distribution, and future-season economics**; EverWonder gets a **fair, defined production deal
on the seasons it makes** — and cannot become the owner of, or sole gatekeeper to, the buyer
relationship or the IP._
