## Key takeaways

1. Official leave-alone policy coexists with Coast Guard/Navy surveillance and 2018 RAP tourism softening.
2. Not absolutely uncontacted — Portman kidnappings, gift missions, Chau 2018, Polyakov 2025.
3. Census population figures are distance guesses; anthropologists estimate higher.
4. No solid public evidence of a secret base/CIA site; Andaman Command interest is archipelago-wide.
5. Darkest documented story is institutional outsider continuum, not a black site.

## Bottom line

The darkest documented story is not a secret lab under the canopy. It is a century-plus continuum of outsiders treating North Sentinel as a canvas — science, salvage, state anthropology, Gospel, tourism branding, GoPro — against a people whose only reliably recorded political speech is arrows and refusal. Military interest in the Andamans as a whole is real and public; Sentinel-as-black-site is fan fiction until someone produces documents.
