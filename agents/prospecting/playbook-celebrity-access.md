# Celebrity-access playbook

**Owner bot:** Prospecting  
**Product:** Making Cinderella / Cinderella Corp  
**ICP:** [icp-making-cinderella.md](./icp-making-cinderella.md)

Public-clean. No phones, emails, iMessage dumps, Dex/NCD, or Network Master PII. Prospect lists stay off-vault or names-only.

---

## Playbook

1. **Deduplicate** against Cinderella Targets **Active / HOT** and **Celebrity_Targets** before adding names.
2. **Target door-openers** (managers / agents), not cold DMs to talent.
3. **Confirm titles** on public pages. Blank contact unless published with a URL. Never pattern-build emails.
4. **Draft the ownership pitch:** no capital from the celebrity; time/effort only; genuinely competitive for a national championship; emotional + financial why.
5. **Never mention** the closed raise / $1.5M / % equity in celebrity outreach.
6. **Norman approves** before any send.
7. **Coordinate with Connections** for warm Network Master intros. Do not invent a parallel CRM.
8. **School-AD lists** are secondary inventory only.
