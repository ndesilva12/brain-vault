## Key sourced facts (do not invent)
- Area: 59.67 km² — A&N Island-wise Statistical Outline 2023–24 (http://andssw1.and.nic.in/ecostat/pdf/IslandwiseStatistical2023-24.pdf)
- Legal: island + ~5 km coastal sea = tribal reserve under A&N Islands (Protection of Aboriginal Tribes) Regulation, 1956 — PIB https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1562728 ; MHA Lok Sabha PDF https://www.mha.gov.in/MHA1/Par2017/pdfs/par2018-pdfs/ls-18122018/1158.pdf
- Policy: “eyes-on and hands-off” — PIB 5 Feb 2019
- Population: ESTIMATE only (~50 official / often cited); offshore counts unreliable; NGO range often ~50–150 — PIB; Survival International
- Landmark contacts: 2006 fishermen killed; Nov 2018 John Allen Chau killed (body recovery abandoned — BBC https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-india-46354940); Mar 2025 influencer Polyakov illegal landing arrested (Survival https://www.survivalinternational.org/peoples/sentinelese)

## Worldview lens (brief)
Consent as revealed preference; contact externalities (disease/demographic collapse of neighboring Andamanese); free association includes refusal; skepticism of civilizing missions and romantic primitivism alike.
