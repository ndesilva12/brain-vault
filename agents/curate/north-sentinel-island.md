## Editorial note
North Sentinel is a live stress-test of property, consent, and sovereignty: India claims the island on paper while the Sentinelese enforce de facto borders with arrows, and neither side can bargain without risking epidemic death. State paternalism and NGO leave-alone orthodoxy both decide for people who cannot be asked, yet missionary contact and influencer stunts treat those same people as props for salvation or content.
