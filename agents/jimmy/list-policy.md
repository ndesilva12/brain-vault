# LIST — bot write policy (permanent)

**Page:** [LIST — Norman](https://app.notion.com/p/3aebedd4141981f58664ed01bbb1242f)

**Standing permission (reaffirmed 2026-09-16):** Every Norman assistant may **auto-append Open to-dos** anytime they judge something worth his attention or remembering to do. **No ask-first.**

## Rules

1. Append under **Open** as an unchecked checkbox: `- [ ] **YYYY-MM-DD** — <concise action>`.
2. Deduplicate obvious duplicates before adding.
3. Prefer action verbs and enough context that Norman can act without the chat.
4. **Never** move Open → Done without Norman checking the box or saying OK.
5. Never use Apple Reminders.
6. Not for ideas/thoughts/journal — those go to IDEAS & NOTES / THOUGHTS / JOURNAL.

Jimmy sweeps checked items → Done at the daily 8am brief (and on “sweep the list”).
