## Brief overview

North Sentinel (~60 km², Andamans) is home to the Sentinelese — self-name, language, and census counts unknown. India’s policy is “eyes on, hands off”: PAT exclusion zone, remote monitoring, no prosecution of islanders for defensive killings. The public “Stone Age uncontacted tribe” frame fails first-principles scrutiny: they have experienced abduction, disease, shipwreck salvage, gift missions, and overflights — and repeatedly choose lethal territorial defense.

## Key takeaways

1. Treat “uncontacted Stone Age tribe” as a media product; reality is armed, adaptive territorial refusal after catastrophic regional contact history.
2. Population, language, and interior life are radically underdetermined — every precise number is a guess (Pandit ~80–100; Survival 50–150).
3. Law that matters is **PAT 1956/2012** (island + 5 km coastal sea; multi-year prison terms), not RAP tourism branding.
4. Lethal encounters (1896, 2006, 2018 Chau) sit beside peaceful 1991 coconut exchanges — responses are contingent.
5. Jarawa highway/tourism outcomes are the empirical argument for Sentinelese isolation.
6. 2025 Polyakov influencer landing stress-tests exclusion the way evangelism did.
7. Great Nicobar mega-development vs Sentinel sanctuary shows selective “hands off” — sovereignty/optics, not a pure rights regime.
8. Best expert posture (Pandit, Survival, field anthro): leave them alone, enforce the buffer, skip body-recovery theater.
